The video wizard takes you from a blank slate to a rendering reel in a single guided flow. Which steps you see depends on the template you pick, but every path starts with a template choice, puts someone on camera, asks where the script comes from, lets you edit that script, and ends with a build step before the render. This page covers every step in detail so you know what each control does and why it's there.

## Template selection

The first screen, "What are we making today?", has two entry points: a **Recreate a reel** field at the top where you paste any Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube link and click **Recreate →**, and three template cards below it.

| Template | Steps | Plan required |
|---|---|---|
| **UGC Reel** | Template → Avatar → Source → Script → Shots | Starter and up |
| **Two-Hander Podcast** | Template → Cast → Source → Script → Studio | Creator and up |
| **Split-Window Reel** | Template → Avatar → Source → Script → B-roll → Shots | Creator and up |

Each card shows the template name, a tagline, and an estimated starting credit cost. Click a card to pick it and advance. A **Continue with UGC Reel →** link in the footer lets you skip straight to the classic reel without clicking a card.

## Avatar and Cast

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="UGC Reel & Split-Window Reel">
    The **Avatar** step shows your avatar library as tiles. Click the avatar you want on camera, then **Continue to source →**. If an avatar tile has no voice yet, the wizard sends you to add one before it can be selected — an avatar without a voice cannot render.

    If an upgrade screen appears after this step, your plan does not yet include video rendering. Pick a plan to continue.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Two-Hander Podcast">
    The **Cast** step fills two seats: host (right) and guest (left). Tap one avatar to assign the host; tap another for the guest. Tapping the currently selected host again clears the pick.

    If you own only one avatar, a "AvatarFactory co-hosts" rail appears and you can seat a house co-host as the guest. **Continue to Source →** stays locked until both seats are filled.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Source

The Source step, "Where does the script come from?", offers four paths.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="AI Content Expert">
    Select **Ideate with AI content expert** (marked Recommended) and the wizard brainstorms six viral hooks matched to your avatar's niche. Pick the one that fits, or type a note in "What didn't land?" and request a fresh batch.

    After you pick a hook, the wizard adds a short **Writing** step — "Writing your script." — where the AI expands the hook into a full draft. The editor opens automatically when the script is ready. **← Pick a different idea** takes you back to the hook list at any time.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reel / TikTok URL">
    Select **Paste a Reel / TikTok URL**, paste an Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube link, and choose how deep the copy goes:

    - **Transcribe** — pulls the spoken words only. Free. The transcript lands in the editor for you to adapt.
    - **✦ Full Clone · 10 cr** — runs a full video analysis: cuts, camera moves, movements, and props, shot by shot. The Shots step then rebuilds the same storyboard with your avatar performing it.

    See [Recreate a Reel](/videos/recreate-a-reel) for the full cloning guide.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Followed Account">
    Select **From a followed account** to browse recent reels from accounts you track, then click **Use ▾** on the reel you want to adapt.

    <Note>
      This tile is locked on the Starter plan. Upgrade to Creator or Influencer to access it.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Write from Scratch">
    Select **Write from scratch** to open a blank editor and type or paste your own script directly.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Script

The Script step shows the source card at the top, the editor in the center, and the Script Co-writer panel on the right. Below the editor, a live word count and a "~N sec at your avatar's pace" estimate update as you type.

**Key controls:**

- **Hear It** — plays the full script in your avatar's voice before you spend any credits. Use this to catch pacing issues or awkward phrasing.
- **Script Co-writer** — propose rewrites without leaving the editor. Three modes:
  - **Rewrite everything** — returns a full rewrite for you to apply or discard.
  - **Get inspiration only** — returns ideas without overwriting your text.
  - **80/20** — focuses on the hook and the first few seconds.
  
  Click **Apply** on a proposal card to use it, or **↺ Revert** to restore your original text at any time. The co-writer never costs credits and is capped at 50 turns per day on a paid plan.
- **Change source** — returns to the Source step.
- **Swap avatar →** — returns to the avatar grid.

In the Two-Hander Podcast template, speaker tags in the script name who says each line. Template flows may also list script rules below the editor; blocking rules disable the **Continue** button until you fix them, and the co-writer offers to fix them for you.

Click **Continue to Shots →** when the script reads right.

## Shots (UGC Reel)

The Shots step, "Build your shot puzzle.", is where the script becomes a visual sequence.

### AI producer's first cut

When the step opens, the AI producer reads the script and directs a first cut automatically. A banner above the shot list shows its progress. Once done, the banner offers:

- **Re-direct All** — ask the producer for another pass on the whole shot list.
- **Undo** — revert to your previous cut.

### Shot cards

Each shot card holds four editable fields:

<Accordion title="Model">
  Choose between:
  - **Talking head** (HeyGen Avatar IV) — avatar faces the camera and speaks, lip-synced. 1 credit/sec. Runs 3–120 seconds.
  - **Cinematic motion** (Kling v3) — camera moves, avatar moves through a scene. 2 credits/sec. Runs 3–10 seconds.

  The cinematic model shows a **🔒 Creator** lock on a Starter plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Scene">
  Each shot needs a scene — the visual environment the avatar appears in. Three options are available after the first shot:

  - **Generate This Scene** — creates a new scene in the scene drawer from a text description.
  - **Continue From Previous Scene** — chains the shot to the last frame of the shot before it, preserving the same set, outfit, and framing. Stays greyed out ("Generate the previous scene first") until the preceding shot has a scene.
  - **Pick an Existing Scene** — select from your saved scenes library.

  On the first shot, only **Generate This Scene** appears. When a chained shot ends without the avatar's face visible (shot from behind, or hands only), the next shot in the chain automatically starts from the scene where the chain began, so the render always has a face to work from.

  The **Producer Suggests** panel describes a scene for every shot that has none yet — on the first shot it offers **Generate This Scene** directly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Direction and Transcript">
  The direction field describes what the avatar does. The transcript field holds the words for this slice of the script. Audio tags in brackets (for example, `[pause]` or `[excited]`) shape delivery and are never spoken aloud or included in captions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Length">
  Talking-head shots run 3–120 seconds. Cinematic shots run 3–10 seconds. The length is derived from the transcript word count but can be adjusted manually.
</Accordion>

### Length meter and subtitles

Above the shot list:

- **Length meter** reads "Video length your plan renders" in green. It turns to "Over the length your plan renders" in red when the cut exceeds your plan's cap. Click **Shorten to Fit** to trim automatically; **Undo** appears for a few seconds if you change your mind.
- **Subtitles** is a single On/Off switch for the whole video. Turning it On reveals "Choose a caption style" with six styles: Studio Pop, Chip, Stamp Drop, Marker Swipe, Mono Bracket, and Typewriter.

### Generating the render

If some shots still need scenes, a panel offers **Generate N scenes** or **Generate scenes + video**, which generates missing scenes and starts the render in one go.

Click **Generate · N cr** in the footer. A confirmation dialog, "Start the render?", lists every shot, the total seconds, the credits the render costs, and what will remain in your balance. Click **Generate · N cr** to start, or **Not yet** to go back and make changes.

## B-roll (Split-Window Reel)

The **B-roll** step cuts your script into 2–5 chapters and matches a video clip plus a music bed to each one.

Each chapter card shows:

- The matched clip from the licensed library.
- **Change** — searches the library for a different clip.
- **Generate** — edits the AI clip description to generate a new option.
- **↺ Our pick** — restores the original suggestion.

Matching takes about a minute. After B-roll, the Shots step for the Split-Window template shows a single decision: choose **Natural backdrop**, one of your saved scenes, or **Generate a scene** as the image behind your avatar's talking window. Then click **Animate →** to render.

## Studio (Two-Hander Podcast)

The **Pick your studio.** screen lists your saved studios, presets, and past episodes.

Click **Generate New Studio** to open the studio agent: describe the room in plain words and the agent writes a brief, asks a clarifying question when it needs one, and returns several empty studio options (the cast is seated in the next step). Click an option to refine it in plain words, then **Use this studio**.

Below the studio picker:

- Add an optional prop behind each person's seat.
- Click **✦ Seat the cast · ~6 scene images** to see both avatars placed in the room.
- Each seat has **↻ Re-roll** to try a different placement.

Click **Animate →** to render once the cast is seated.

## The oven screen

After you confirm the render, the oven screen appears: "Your video is in the oven." Three options let you decide what to do while it processes:

- **Run in background →** — closes the oven and returns you to the Videos page.
- **Go To Videos** — navigates directly to the Videos tab.
- **+ Start Your Next Video** — opens the wizard again immediately.

You'll receive an email and an in-app notification when the video is ready. The finished reel appears on the Videos page, and the You Shipped screen offers **Download**.

## Drafts and the step guard

The wizard saves your draft on every step change and after each pause in typing. To find unfinished work:

- Open **Videos** and click the **Drafts** tab.
- Or visit the wizard directly — a resume banner offers to pick up your newest draft.

<Info>
  A step cannot open before the steps it depends on are complete. If you refresh on the Shots step with no script saved, the wizard returns you to Source. This is the **step guard** — your draft is not lost. Pick it up from the Drafts tab and the wizard will land you on the earliest step that needs attention.
</Info>
