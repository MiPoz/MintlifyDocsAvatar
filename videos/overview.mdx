The Videos section of Avatar Factory is where scripts become published-ready short-form content. You pick a template, assign an avatar, source or write a script, build a shot list, and send the whole thing to render. Twenty minutes later an MP4 reel lands in your library, captioned and ready to post — no camera, no studio, no editing software required.

## How the wizard works

Every video follows the same spine: pick a template → assign who is on camera → choose a script source → edit the script → build the shots or studio → render. The wizard saves your draft on every step, so you can close the browser and return to exactly where you left off from the **Drafts** tab on the Videos page.

Rendering runs entirely on Avatar Factory's servers. Once you confirm the render you can close the tab; you'll receive an email and an in-app notification the moment the video is ready.

## Three templates

Each template produces a distinct video format. The wizard's opening screen, "What are we making today?", shows all three as cards.

| Template | What it makes | Plan required |
|---|---|---|
| **UGC Reel** | Single-avatar talking-to-camera reel with cinematic motion options | Starter and up |
| **Two-Hander Podcast** | Two-avatar podcast in a generated studio | Creator and up |
| **Split-Window Reel** | Avatar talking-window with AI B-roll chapters and a music bed | Creator and up |

<Note>
  If a template card shows a **Creator+** pill, selecting it on a Starter plan opens an upgrade prompt. Upgrade first to continue with that template.
</Note>

## Two shot models

Every shot in a UGC Reel or Split-Window Reel uses one of two rendering engines. The wizard's model menu names them on each shot card.

| Model | What it does | Cost | Length range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HeyGen Avatar IV** | Talking head — avatar faces the camera and speaks the transcript, lip-synced | 1 credit / second | 3 – 120 seconds |
| **Kling v3** | Cinematic motion — camera moves, avatar moves through a scene | 2 credits / second | 3 – 10 seconds |

Voice is included in both rates. The credit cost of a full render is the sum of each shot's rate multiplied by its length in seconds. The wizard shows the exact number on the **Generate · N cr** button before you confirm.

<Warning>
  The **Starter plan** renders HeyGen Avatar IV (talking-head) shots only and caps each video at **45 seconds**. Upgrade to Creator or Influencer to unlock cinematic shots and remove the length cap.
</Warning>

## How long renders take

Most videos finish in **10 to 20 minutes**. Longer videos with multiple cinematic shots can take more. While the render runs, the video card on the Videos page shows the current stage and a countdown estimate. You can close the browser — the work continues on Avatar Factory's servers.

Factors that extend render time:

- More shots and longer total runtime
- Cinematic (Kling v3) shots, which take longer per second than talking-head shots
- Scenes that still need generating before the render can start
- Caption burning (the last stage when Subtitles is On)

After the render finishes, adding or changing a caption style on the video page is a separate shorter job — about a minute for the first burn, seconds for styles after that.
