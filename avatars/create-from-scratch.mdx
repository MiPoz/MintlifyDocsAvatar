Building from scratch gives you complete creative control over your avatar. You start with a blank description and shape every detail — the character's look, origin, vibe, life story, and voice — before a single image is generated. The four-step wizard (Identity → Story → Face → Voice) walks you through it all, and a **Save & exit** button in the top bar saves a draft at any point so you can pick back up later.

To open the wizard, go to **Avatars** in the sidebar, click **+ New Avatar**, and choose **Start from scratch**.

## The four wizard steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identity — Describe your character">
    The heading reads **Describe your character from scratch**. You need to fill in at least one of: a description, a reference image, or a category.

    1. **Write a description.** Click any chip — **Age**, **Gender**, **Country**, **Vibe**, **Look**, or others — to add that label to the box, then type the details. Example: *"Age: 38. Gender: male. Country: Russia, lives in Miami. Vibe: confident, slightly arrogant. Look: black turtleneck, gold chain, grey beard."* The **Description strength** meter fills as you cover more areas.
    2. **Add reference images (optional).** Upload up to six files, or paste links from Instagram, Pinterest, a blog, or a direct image URL. The toggle above the grid sets the mode:
       - **Inspiration** — borrows the style or mood from your references; the generated faces won't copy any specific person.
       - **Exact match** — uses your photo as the face itself, unchanged. Your upload appears as a **Your Uploads · Use As-Is** row on the Face step.
    3. **Set a category (optional).** Pre-filled from your onboarding niche. Leave it as **Other** and content is written from what's going viral instead of a fixed category.
    4. Click **Continue to story →**.

    <Tip>
      Specific beats vague. Cover gender, country or origin, profession or niche, accent, look and wardrobe, and vibe. A few concrete lines beat a long paragraph of adjectives.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Story — Write the backstory and audience">
    A strong backstory shapes the scripts your avatar delivers. It doesn't need to be long — just grounded.

    1. **Backstory.** Write it yourself, or click **Ideate for me** to generate three different angles. Click **Use this →** on the one that fits. A good backstory opens with a concrete fact, adds a turning point, names what the character refuses to do, and stays under about 150 words.
    2. **Target audience.** Click **Generate** for a draft, or type it manually using the chips (Age, Gender, Interest, Income / life stage).
    3. Click **Continue to face previews →**.

    <Note>
      Clicking **Continue to face previews →** starts face generation and charges one credit per face — three credits total for the first set.
    </Note>

    Signature phrases and topics to avoid are **not** part of the wizard. You add them on the avatar page after saving.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Face — Choose, refine, or generate more">
    Three face tiles render one by one with a countdown. This step is also where you name your avatar.

    - **Name field.** Type the avatar's name, or click **Brainstorm 5 names** for suggestions.
    - **Choose.** Click **Choose** on a tile to select it. The button changes to **✓ Picked**.
    - **Refine.** Click **Refine** to open the refinement sheet. Pick chips — **Hair**, **Outfit**, **Background**, **Lighting**, **Expression** — and add a note. Click **Apply to This Face**. The refined result is added as a new card labeled "Refined" next to the original, so you never lose the base. **1 credit per refine.**
    - **Reroll.** Generates a fresh variation of that face from the same brief.
    - **Generate 3 more.** Confirms the cost (3 credits) and starts a new set of three. All sets stay accessible via the set switcher above the grid.

    In **Exact match** mode, your uploaded photo appears in a **Your Uploads · Use As-Is** row above the generated faces. Choose it to use the photo unchanged.

    When a tile shows "Couldn't generate this face", click **Try again** on that tile. Each retry costs 1 credit. If all three fail, see the [generation failures guide](/troubleshooting/generation-failures).

    Click **Continue to voice →** once you've picked a face.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Voice — Design and audition three previews">
    The avatar's voice is used in every video it appears in.

    1. The **Voice description** drafts itself automatically from the face, story, and references. Edit it freely, or click **Regenerate prompt** for a new draft. The field requires at least 10 characters.
    2. **Optional:** open **Advanced · tuning & preview text** to set **Loudness**, **Guidance**, and a custom line for the previews to speak (up to 200 characters).
    3. Click **Generate 3 voice previews** — 1 credit for the batch of three.
    4. Press play on each tile. Click **Use this voice** on the one that sounds like a real person without trying.
    5. To tweak a single tile, type a note ("warmer, slower") under it and click **Regenerate** (⅓ credit). **Generate 3 more** gives a fresh batch (1 credit).
    6. Click **Finish avatar →** — or **Save without voice** to add one later from the avatar page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Credit cost summary

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| 3 face candidates (first set) | 3 cr |
| Each additional face refine or reroll | 1 cr |
| Voice batch (3 previews) | 1 cr |
| Single voice regenerate | ⅓ cr |
| Reference Pack (saved automatically on finish) | 6 cr |
| **Typical complete build** | **~10 cr** |

<Note>
  The Reference Pack — six angles of your chosen face — is built automatically when you save. It charges 6 credits and runs in the background after you click **Finish avatar →**.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Inspiration and Exact match?">
    **Inspiration** treats your reference images as a mood board: the generated faces borrow the style and feel, not the specific person. **Exact match** uses your photo as the face itself, unchanged, and displays it in a **Your Uploads · Use As-Is** row on the Face step. Use Exact match when you already have a character and want that precise face — for example, when importing your own likeness.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What makes a good character description?">
    Specific beats vague. Cover gender, country or origin, the profession or niche the character talks about, an accent if they have one, their look and wardrobe, and their vibe. The chips above the description box add each label for you. A few concrete lines beat a long paragraph of adjectives — think *"34-year-old Nigerian woman, Lagos-born, now in London, speaks fast and sharp, minimal jewelry, always in tailored blazers"* rather than *"a stylish, smart, beautiful woman"*.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I save a draft and come back later?">
    Yes. Click **Save & exit** in the top bar at any step. The next time you open **+ New Avatar**, you'll see a **Continue a Draft** prompt. Drafts also appear under the **Drafts** tab on the Avatars page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
