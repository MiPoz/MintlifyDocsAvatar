Avatars are the heart of Avatar Factory. Each avatar is a fully realized AI character — complete with a generated (or imported) face, a designed voice, a written backstory, and a library of scenes and photos. Once you build one, you never have to film anything: the avatar appears on camera in every video you render, speaking your script in its own voice, in whatever setting you choose.

## What an avatar is made of

Every avatar has four core parts built through the creation wizard, plus a supporting asset called the Reference Pack.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Identity" icon="id-card">
    A text description of the character — age, gender, country, vibe, look — plus optional reference images and a content category. This brief drives everything that comes after.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Story" icon="book-open">
    A backstory (who the character is, their turning point, what they refuse) and a target audience definition. These power the AI-written scripts your avatar delivers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Face" icon="face-smile">
    A generated or imported portrait. Three candidates render from the Identity description; you choose, refine, or reroll until you have the face you want.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Voice" icon="microphone">
    A designed AI voice, generated from a text description of gender, origin, accent, pacing, and tone — or a voice pulled from your connected ElevenLabs account.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### The Reference Pack

When you save an avatar, Avatar Factory automatically builds a **Reference Pack**: six angles of the chosen face rendered in the background. The six shots are the front, left side profile, right side profile, back of the head, a face close-up, and a full body. Every scene and video you generate later draws from this pack to keep the likeness stable across settings, lighting, and camera angles.

## What you can do on the avatar page

After saving, the avatar page becomes the hub for everything related to that character. From here you can:

- Edit the **backstory**, **signature phrases**, and **topics to avoid** that shape every script the avatar delivers
- Open the **Image Studio** to generate new scenes, photos, and outfits for the avatar's library
- Browse the **Videos** tab to see every reel this avatar has appeared in
- Manage the **Reference Pack** — regenerate any angle or refresh the pack for older avatars
- **Edit voice** to redesign the avatar's voice or switch to a connected ElevenLabs voice
