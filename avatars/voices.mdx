Every video your avatar appears in is spoken in its voice. You can set a voice during the avatar creation wizard, when importing a face, or any time afterward by opening the avatar page and clicking **Edit voice**. Avatar Factory generates AI voices from plain text descriptions — specify gender, origin, accent, pacing, pitch, and tone, and get three audible previews to choose from. If you already run a voice in ElevenLabs, you can connect that account and use any voice from your library instead.

## Where to set or change a voice

A voice can be set or changed in three places:

- **The creation wizard** (scratch or template) — the Voice step at the end
- **The import wizard** — the Voice step at the end
- **The avatar page** — click **Edit voice** at any time after saving

If you try to make a video with an avatar that has no voice, the app stops and asks you to add one. Click **Generate Voice** and you land directly on the Voice step — it takes about 30 seconds.

## Design a voice from a description

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write a voice description">
    The **Voice description** field drafts itself automatically from the avatar's face, story, and references. Edit it freely, click **Suggest with AI** if the field is empty, or click **Regenerate prompt** for another AI-written draft. The field requires at least 10 characters.

    Make it specific. The most useful details are:
    - **Gender** — male, female, non-binary
    - **Origin** — *"Emirati from Dubai"* beats *"Middle Eastern"*; *"Nigerian raised in London"* beats *"British"*
    - **Accent** — name it directly
    - **Pacing** — fast and energetic, measured, slow and deliberate
    - **Pitch** — deep baritone, mid-range, high and bright
    - **Timbre** — pick two or three words: warm, gravelly, smooth, breathy, crisp, resonant
    - **Emotion or persona** — authoritative, playful, conspiratorial, calm
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optional: open Advanced settings">
    Click **Advanced · tuning & preview text** to access:
    - **Loudness** — overall volume of the voice
    - **Guidance** — how closely the model follows the description
    - **Custom preview text** — a specific line (up to 200 characters) for the previews to speak, instead of the default
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate 3 voice previews">
    Click **Generate 3 voice previews**. This charges **1 credit** for the batch of three. The previews render in about 30 seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a voice">
    Press play on **Voice 1**, **Voice 2**, and **Voice 3**. Click **Use this voice** on the one that sounds like a real person without trying. The button changes to **✓ Picked**.

    <Tip>
      If none of the three convince you, don't settle. Regenerate a fresh batch rather than picking the least-bad option. Voice carries a lot of the realism.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Finish avatar →** (or **Save avatar →** in the import wizard) to save the voice with the avatar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Regenerate a preview

You don't have to commit to the first batch. Two regeneration options are available:

- **Single tile.** Under any preview tile, type what should change — *"warmer, slower, less announcer"* — and click **Regenerate**. This costs **⅓ credit** and returns one updated preview.
- **Fresh batch of 3.** Click **Generate 3 more** for an entirely new set of three previews. This costs **1 credit**. The new three replace the current grid, so pick a favorite from the current batch before you replace them if one was close.

<Note>
  If you see *"Pick one of the three voices first."*, play the previews, click **Use this voice** on one, then try the action again.
</Note>

## Change the voice after saving

1. Open the avatar from **Avatars** and click **Edit voice**.
2. The Voice step opens with your current voice sample shown at the top.
3. Generate new previews and click **Use this voice**, or switch to your ElevenLabs voice list.
4. Click **Finish avatar →** to save the change.

Previously rendered videos keep the voice they were made with. Only new videos use the updated voice.

## Connect your ElevenLabs account

If you already have a voice in ElevenLabs — a cloned voice, a custom design, or a licensed voice — connect your account and use it in Avatar Factory. Speech for avatars using an ElevenLabs voice runs on your ElevenLabs credits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the account">
    1. Open **Settings**, scroll to **Connected Accounts**, and click **Connect** next to **ElevenLabs**. (The import wizard's **Use my ElevenLabs voice** panel has the same form.)
    2. In ElevenLabs, click **Create API Key** and give it a name.
    3. Set these four permissions on the key:
       - **Text to Speech** — enabled ✓
       - **Speech to Speech** — enabled ✓
       - **Voices** — Read
       - **User** — Access
    4. Copy the key (it starts with `sk_`) and paste it into the box in Avatar Factory. Press Enter or click the connect button.

    <Note>
      If you see *"That key didn't verify. Check it and try again."*, the key is either missing a permission or lost a character during the paste. Create a fresh key with all four permissions and paste it again.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the voice">
    1. On the Voice step, choose **Use my ElevenLabs voice**.
    2. Use **Search your voices** to find the one you want. Each row has a play button to preview it.
    3. Click the row to select it, then save the avatar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Reconnect or disconnect

In **Settings**, the ElevenLabs row shows **Connected** or **Not connected**. If a stored key stops working, click **Reconnect** and paste a fresh key. Clicking **Disconnect** removes the key — the confirmation explains that avatars using those voices will need a reconnect before their next video.

## Credit reference

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Generate 3 voice previews | 1 cr |
| Regenerate a single preview with a note | ⅓ cr |
| Generate a fresh batch of 3 | 1 cr |
| Speech in videos | Included in per-second video rate |
| ElevenLabs voice speech | Runs on your ElevenLabs credits |

<Warning>
  Canceling your Avatar Factory subscription releases the voices you designed in Avatar Factory, and they cannot be recovered. If you plan to return, **pause your subscription** instead of canceling. See the [subscription management guide](/billing/manage-subscription) for instructions.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does a voice cost in credits?">
    One credit for a batch of three previews, and one-third of a credit to regenerate a single preview with a note. Saving the avatar with the chosen voice costs nothing extra. Speech in your videos is included in the per-second video rate, so you're not charged again for the voice when you render.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the voice after the avatar is saved?">
    Yes. Open the avatar page and click **Edit voice**. It reopens the Voice step with your current voice shown, and you can generate new previews and pick one, or switch to a connected ElevenLabs voice. Videos you've already rendered keep the voice they were made with.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my ElevenLabs voice list is empty?">
    If the list says *"No voices in this ElevenLabs account yet"*, the connected account has no voices. Create one at elevenlabs.io, or switch to **✦ Design a new voice** in Avatar Factory.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
