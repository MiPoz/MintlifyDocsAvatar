Templates are the fastest way to get a high-quality avatar without starting from a blank page. Each template is a ready-made character brief — a description, reference images, a content category, a backstory, and a target audience — built for a specific niche and persona. Picking one opens the same four-step wizard as building from scratch, but every field arrives pre-filled. Change what you want, leave what works, and generate the face and voice when you're ready. Nothing is created (and no credits are charged) until you move past the Story step.

## Find a template

### Featured templates

1. Open **Avatars** in the sidebar and click **+ New Avatar**.
2. On **How do you want to build it?**, scroll down to the **Featured templates** row. Four cards appear, with templates from your onboarding niche shown first.
3. Each card displays the niche, character name, a short blurb, and chips for gender, age range, and origin.
4. Click any card to open the wizard with that template loaded.

### Browse the full library

1. Under the featured row, click **See all templates**.
2. Use the **niche tabs** at the top to filter:

3. Search by name or mood using **Search templates by niche, vibe, persona…**
4. Sort with **Most popular**, **Name A to Z**, or **Name Z to A**.
5. Hover a card and click **Use →**, or click the card and then **Use this template** in the footer bar.

<Tip>
  If nothing in the featured row matches your niche, click **See all templates** and use the niche tabs to filter directly to your category — every canonical niche has a tab.
</Tip>

## Customize the template in the wizard

The Identity step opens as **Customize your template** with the note *"Every field below is pre-filled from the template. Change anything."* From there, it follows the standard wizard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identity — Edit the pre-filled character brief">
    Review the description, reference images, and category. Edit or replace anything. Swap out reference images, add up to six total, and adjust the description chips. Click **Continue to story →**.

    <Tip>
      Edit the description **before** the Face step — faces are generated from it. Changing the description after faces have been generated means a new batch and new credits.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Story — Refine the backstory and audience">
    The backstory and target audience are pre-filled from the template. Rewrite them, or click **Ideate for me** for fresh angles. Click **Continue to face previews →** when ready.

    <Note>
      Clicking **Continue to face previews →** starts face generation and charges 1 credit per face (3 credits for the first set of three).
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Face — Pick, refine, or generate more">
    Three face tiles render from the template's description. Name the avatar in the **Name** field here — the template's name is a placeholder; rename it to something that fits your character. **Choose** a tile, **Refine** it with chips (Hair, Outfit, Background, Lighting, Expression), or **Generate 3 more** for a fresh set. Click **Continue to voice →**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Voice — Audition and pick">
    Generate 3 voice previews (1 credit), listen to each, and click **Use this voice**. Click **Finish avatar →** to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a detailed explanation of every button on each step, see [Create from Scratch](/avatars/create-from-scratch) — the same controls apply.

## Tips for getting the most from templates

- **Rename the avatar.** The template name is a placeholder. The name field and breadcrumbs update in real time as you type on the Face step.
- **Keep the category unless you're changing niches.** The category drives the featured content and followed accounts the avatar sees later. Only change it if you're intentionally moving the character to a new niche.
- **Edit the description before the Face step.** Faces are generated from the description. A change afterwards means starting a new batch.
- **Use Exact match to keep the template's look with your own face.** Add your photo as a reference image and set the toggle to **Exact match** on the Identity step. Or go back and choose **Import your avatar** instead — see [Import Your Own Face](/avatars/import-your-face).

<Info>
  Two people who pick the same template and change nothing still get different generated faces. Templates share the brief, not the output.
</Info>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a template copy an existing avatar?">
    No. A template is a starting brief, not a clone of another user's avatar. It pre-fills the description, reference images, category, and story, and you can edit any of it before a face is generated. Every person who uses the same template generates their own unique faces, and the name, voice, and backstory are yours to set.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see my niche in the featured row?">
    The featured row shows four templates, with your onboarding niche first when a match exists. If none of the four match, the row falls back to the default order. Click **See all templates**, then use the niche tabs to filter to your category — every niche has a dedicated tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if no template fits what I want?">
    Click the **Build from scratch** card in the template browser. It takes you directly to the empty wizard where you write the entire character brief yourself. See [Create from Scratch](/avatars/create-from-scratch) for the full walkthrough.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
