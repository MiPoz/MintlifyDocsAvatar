Scenes are the still images your videos are built from. Every new setting in a video — the penthouse at dusk, the trading desk at night, the yacht deck — needs a scene saved to the avatar's library. The Image Studio is where you create those scenes, plus portrait photos for thumbnails and posts, and outfits the avatar can wear in any image. You describe what you want in plain language, get back a set of options, edit the ones you like by describing changes, and save the keepers to the library. The Shots step of the video wizard then picks from whatever you've saved.

<Info>
  Scenes and videos are part of your paid plan. On the $1 trial you can build avatars, but generating scenes and videos requires an active paid subscription.
</Info>

## Open the Image Studio

1. Open an avatar from **Avatars**.
2. Click the **Scenes** or **Photos** tab, then click **Generate scene**, **Generate photo**, or the dashed tile at the start of the grid.
3. The studio opens with a fresh conversation. Each visit starts a new conversation — pictures you've saved stay in the library regardless.

## Generate a scene or photo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the setting">
    Type your description in the message box. Location, time of day, lighting, and one or two props work best — for example: *"Home office at night, six ultrawide monitors, the glow of red and green tickers on the face."* You can also click one of the idea chips above the box to fill it with a niche-appropriate suggestion.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the generation options (pills)">
    Before sending, configure the pills under the box:

    - **Options** — how many images to generate per ask: 1, 2, 3, or 4. Each option costs 1 credit.
    - **Outfit** — **Auto** lets the description decide the clothes. Selecting one of the avatar's saved outfits applies it to every option regardless of what the text says.
    - **Format** — **Scene** (vertical, for videos) or **Photo** (portrait, for posts and thumbnails). The heading and placeholder text in the box update to match.
    - **Image model** — the AI model used for generation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send and review options">
    Click send. If your description is too thin to generate from, the studio asks one short clarifying question first — that exchange is free. Otherwise, generation starts and options appear as tiles.

    <Tip>
      If the studio asks a clarifying question, answer it directly. The follow-up question is free, and the extra context produces noticeably better options.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add reference images

Click **+** next to the message box to attach up to six reference images: **Upload From Device**, or pick one from the avatar's library. You can also drag and drop or paste images directly into the box. The generated options follow the reference style — a pose, a location, a lighting look — while keeping the avatar's face. Remove a reference with **×** before sending.

## Edit an option

Click any option tile to target it for editing. The message box updates to show *"Editing option 2 · Returns 1 updated image."* Describe the change you want — *"warmer light, hair down, frame tighter"* — and send. You get back one updated version of that specific option, with the same face and everything else kept. Each edit costs **1 credit**. Describe changes iteratively until the image is right.

**Try again** under a reply re-runs the exact same ask without any additional description — useful when you liked the direction but the output missed.

## Save to the library

Pick an option and click **Save to [avatar]'s library**. The confirmation bar reads *"Saved to [avatar]'s scenes · View"* — click **View** to open the library tab with the new image marked **New**. Click **Undo** to reverse a save.

Options you generated but didn't save wait under **Unsorted renders** on the Scenes and Photos tabs. You're never charged for a render that disappears — unsaved images stay accessible until you clear them.

## Outfits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Outfits tab">
    On the avatar page, click the **Outfits** tab and then **Add outfit**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Describe the outfit">
    The outfit wizard opens with niche-appropriate ideas. Describe the clothes: *"white linen shirt, beige chinos, brown loafers"* or *"shirtless, only black jeans and white sneakers."* Options render as the outfit alone on a white background — every garment you named, nothing you left out, no person.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and use">
    Pick one and click **Save to [avatar]'s outfits**. It appears first in the Outfits tab and becomes available from the **Outfit** pill whenever you generate a scene or photo.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add your own images

Already have a photo of the avatar — or a setting you want to use? On the avatar page, click **Add Your Own** to save a file or paste a link directly to the library. No generation happens and no credits are charged.

## The Reference Pack

Saving an avatar automatically builds a **Reference Pack**: six angles of the chosen face that keep the likeness stable across all scenes and videos. The six angles are:

| Angle | Description |
|---|---|
| Front | The base image; all other angles are drawn from it |
| Left side profile | Left-facing profile |
| Right side profile | Right-facing profile |
| Back of head | Shows the hairstyle from behind |
| Face close-up | Detailed facial shot |
| Full body | Avatar in plain, fitted sportswear to show build, arms, and legs |

Every angle except the full body shares the same hairstyle and outfit as the front. View the pack on the avatar page under **Reference Pack**.

To regenerate a single angle, click **Regenerate this angle** — **1 credit per image**. An angle that fails is not charged. Because every angle is drawn from the front, regenerate the front image first if it needs updating.

For avatars created before the face close-up existed, you'll see a **Close-Up of Eyes** angle instead. Click **Refresh Face & Body** to replace it with the current face close-up and a fresh full body — 1 credit per image, 2 credits total.

## When a scene doesn't look right

- **Face drifted:** Add more reference images of the same person to the Reference Pack, then regenerate the scene.
- **Setting is wrong:** Click the option and describe the change instead of starting over. It keeps everything that worked and costs 1 credit.
- **Generation failed or timed out:** See the [generation failures guide](/troubleshooting/generation-failures).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a scene and a photo?">
    Both are generated images of your avatar. A **scene** is a vertical still shot sized for video — it's saved to the Scenes tab and available in the Shots step of the video wizard. A **photo** is a portrait image for posts and thumbnails, saved to the Photos tab. The Image Studio makes both; switch between them with the **Format** pill.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many credits does one picture cost?">
    One credit per option. Asking for four options at once costs four credits; asking for one costs one. Editing an option returns one updated image for one credit. A clarifying question from the studio costs nothing. Options you don't save still count as used, but they wait under **Unsorted renders** so nothing you paid for is lost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the face drift in my scene?">
    Face drift happens when the scene generator doesn't have enough reference material to lock the likeness. Add more angles or expressions to the avatar's **Reference Pack** — the more reference coverage it has, the more consistent the face stays across different settings. Then regenerate the scene.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
