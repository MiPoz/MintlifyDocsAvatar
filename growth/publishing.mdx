Video creation in Avatar Factory is live end to end — you can build an avatar, render a reel, and have a finished 1080×1920 MP4 ready to post. Publishing, however, is a manual step today: you download the file and post it to Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts yourself. The app contains pages for scheduled posts, auto-publishing, and campaigns, but those pages are labeled **Coming Soon** or **Beta** and do not act on your account yet. This article explains exactly what works now and what doesn't.

## How to Post Today

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download your finished video">
    Open the video page for your completed reel. Click **⬇ With Captions…** to download the MP4 with burned-in captions, or **⬇ Clean Master** to download the version without captions. Both export as a 1080×1920 MP4.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and copy your caption">
    Find the **Social media description** panel on the same video page. Click **Edit**, then either click **Generate** to let Avatar Factory write the caption or type your own. Click **Save** — a copy button appears next to the saved caption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post on your platform of choice">
    Open Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts. Upload the MP4 you downloaded, paste the caption, and post. The **⋯ More** menu on the video page also offers **Download Thumbnail** and **Copy Link** if you need them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's Labeled "Coming Soon"

The following features are visible in the app but not yet functional:

- **Publishing card on every video page** — Instagram and TikTok rows each read "connection coming soon." The hover text confirms: "Connecting Instagram is coming soon." The same applies to TikTok.
- **Publish buttons on the You Shipped screen** — after a render completes, the Instagram and TikTok publish buttons are disabled and carry a **Soon** pill. The **Download** button on the same screen works normally.
- **Posts page** (labeled "Publishing" in the sidebar) — opens to "No posts scheduled." with the message "Instagram and TikTok connections are coming soon." The **Connect socials · Coming soon** button is not clickable. **Make a video first →** opens the wizard.

## What's Labeled "Beta" (Sample Data Only)

Two additional pages exist in the app but are hidden from the sidebar and show sample data only:

<Accordion title="Auto-Publishing (Automation page)">
  The Automation page shows a "systems running" summary with per-avatar schedules and **Auto-publish** toggles. Every row is labeled "Sample Avatar 1" through "Sample Avatar 5." The toggles, **Configure new schedule**, **Approve →**, and **Approve all** buttons do not run anything on your account. This is a preview of the feature, not a working control panel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Campaigns (Campaign Studio)">
  Campaign Studio carries a **★ Beta** badge and describes batch-generating one script across multiple avatars and scenes. The three campaigns shown are samples. Clicking **+ New campaign** does not start a campaign.
</Accordion>

<Info>
  The sidebar hides the Posts, Automation, and Campaign Studio entries while these features are in this state. You can reach the pages directly by URL, but nothing on them changes your videos, credits, or avatars.
</Info>

## What to Do Right Now

While you're waiting for direct publishing to launch, here's how to keep momentum:

- **Build a posting rhythm in the wizard.** Use **+ Start Your Next Video** on the oven screen to queue your next reel while the current render runs in the background.
- **Use the QA report before every post.** Address Required Fixes first, then work through Recommendations for the cleanest possible result.
- **Use Reel Analytics to pick what to make next.** Sort by Views / Hr to find what's climbing now, then copy the reel or transcript into the wizard.
- **Let the caption generator do the work.** Every video page generates a platform-ready description — copy it before you head to the app.

<Warning>
  No release dates are promised for auto-publishing, scheduling, or campaigns. When any of these features go live, the sidebar entries return, the Publishing card updates, and this article is updated in the same release. Until then, treat any **Soon** or **Beta** label as exactly that.
</Warning>
