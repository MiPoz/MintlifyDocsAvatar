Viral Radar gives you a live window into what your competitors are posting and how their content is performing. It lives in the sidebar as two pages — **Viral Accounts** (your tracked competitors) and **Viral Reels** (analytics across all indexed content) — and it runs on a daily scan budget that automatically adjusts for active versus quiet accounts. Tracking requires a Creator or higher plan; on Starter, both pages open on an upgrade prompt.

## What Viral Radar Does

Tracking an account does three things automatically. It pulls the account's last 50 reels and indexes every hook, format, and hashtag. It rescans on a rolling schedule and tracks 7-day velocity and follower growth. And it surfaces the reels that are climbing fastest so you can copy the pattern into your own wizard.

<Info>
  Viral Radar requires a **Creator or Influencer** plan. Starter customers see an upgrade prompt on both Viral Accounts and Viral Reels.
</Info>

## Track a New Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Viral Accounts and click + Track New Account">
    Find **Viral Accounts** in the sidebar under Viral Radar. On an empty page, type directly into the field and click **Track**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the platform">
    Select **Instagram** or **TikTok** from the picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the username or URL">
    Type the handle or paste the full profile URL and confirm. Handles use letters, numbers, dots, and underscores up to 40 characters — anything outside that format is rejected before the request is sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The first scan runs in the background. The account appears in your list immediately, with **Last Scan** filling in as the reels index.

## Scan Schedule

New accounts start on a fast scan schedule. If posting slows down, the schedule adjusts automatically:

- **Active** — scanned on the standard fast schedule
- **Quiet · checked every 3 days** — no new post for one week
- **Quiet · checked weekly** — no new post for two weeks
- **Quiet · checked monthly** — no new post for one month

As soon as the next scan finds a new reel, the account returns to the fast schedule immediately.

## Viral Radar Limits

One number governs both how many accounts you can track and how many manual rescans you can run per day — whichever limit you hit first.

| Plan | Accounts and Daily Scans |
|---|---|
| Starter | Not included |
| Creator | 10 |
| Influencer (base) | 30 |
| Influencer 2,490 cr tier | 50 |
| Influencer 3,990 cr tier | 70 |
| Influencer 5,990 cr tier | 90 |

<Tip>
  The empty-state page reads "Track up to 50 Instagram or TikTok accounts." That describes the feature ceiling, not your personal allowance. Your actual limit is the number in the table above for your plan. When you reach it, the app shows your own numbers: "You're tracking 10 of 10 Viral Radar accounts. Upgrade to add more."
</Tip>

## Reading the Viral Accounts Page

The header tabs let you slice the list:

- **All** — every tracked account, yours and curated Verified accounts
- **My Follows** — only the accounts you added
- **✦ Verified** — accounts curated and refreshed by Avatar Factory (read-only for you)
- **☆ Favorites** — accounts you starred

Below the tabs, a search box, a **Category** filter, a **Save as default** button, and a sort control round out the toolbar.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Table View">
    The default layout on a laptop. Columns are sortable: **7-Day Growth**, **Followers**, **Reels Indexed**, and **Last Scan**. Click any column header to sort.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cards View">
    Each account appears as a tile showing the handle, bio, Followers, Reels Indexed, 7-day growth, and a **View reels →** button. Cards is always the layout on phones.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## The Rescan Button

The **Rescan** button in the header opens a dialog to select which of your followed accounts to refresh. The counter next to the button shows your remaining daily budget — for example, **30/30**. Hover for the tooltip "N of 30 scans left today." When the budget hits zero, the tooltip reads "No scans left today, resets tomorrow."

Each individual account row also has its own **Rescan** button (counts against the daily budget) and an **Archive** button to remove the account from your list.

<Note>
  Verified accounts cannot be rescanned or archived by you. They refresh on Avatar Factory's own schedule.
</Note>

## Drill Into One Account

Click **View reels →** on any account tile or row to open that account's dedicated page. You'll find:

- A **tracking strip** showing tracking since, reels indexed, last scanned, and total scan count
- **Four KPI tiles** that each drive their own chart: **Followers**, **Total Views**, **Avg Views / Reel**, and **Avg Engagement**
- A **time-range selector**: 7 Days, 30 Days, 3 Months, or All Time
- The account's full reel library with the same filters, sort controls, and **⧉ Copy Reel** action as Viral Reels

<Note>
  A freshly tracked account shows "Not enough data to draw a chart yet." until the second scan completes. Charts need at least two data points.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Sort by **Views / Hr** to find reels that are climbing right now — not reels that already peaked weeks ago.
</Tip>

## When a Scan Fails

An account with a scan error shows a warning on its row with the last attempt time. Click **Rescan** once. If it keeps failing, the account may have gone private, been renamed, or been removed from the platform. Open the profile from the link on its row and check. If the profile is still public and rescans keep failing, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with the handle.
