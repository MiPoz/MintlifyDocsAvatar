# Your first video, from avatar to render

From a saved avatar to a rendering reel: pick the avatar, choose where the script comes from, edit it, set the shots, and start the render on your paid plan.

Getting started · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 3 min read

## Before you start

You need two things: a saved avatar with a voice, and a paid plan. The trial builds the avatar and the plan renders the video. If your avatar has no voice yet, the wizard sends you to a page that says the avatar needs a voice. Click **Generate Voice** there and pick one. It takes about 30 seconds. See [Voices](https://avatarfactory.io/help/voices).

For what the finished output looks like, the [script to video](https://avatarfactory.io/ai-avatar-generator) page shows examples of both shot types.

## Step 1: Avatar

1. Open **Generate** from the sidebar, or click **Make your first video** on the avatar done screen, or **Generate video** on the avatar page.
2. Pick the avatar tile. Go with the one that matches the script you have in mind.
3. Click **Continue to source →**.

## Step 2: Source

This is where the script comes from. Four cards:

- **Ideate with AI content expert.** The AI brainstorms six viral hooks for your avatar and niche. Pick one and you get a draft script.
- **Reel / TikTok URL.** Paste an Instagram, TikTok or YouTube link and click **Transcribe**. The transcript becomes your starting script and is saved to Reel Analytics.
- **Followed accounts.** Pick a reel from an account you follow and click **Use ▾**. Available on Creator and Influencer.
- **Written from scratch.** Skip straight to an empty script.

For a first video, **Ideate with AI content expert** is the quickest. Pick a hook and click **Continue to script →**.

## Step 3: Script

The script editor shows your draft. Edit it as you like, or use the co-writer on the side to rewrite everything or get inspiration only. Read it out loud once: this is exactly what the avatar will say.

Click **Continue** when the text is ready. A disabled button means the editor is still empty: “Write or paste a script to continue”.

## Step 4: Shots

The wizard splits your script into shots. Each shot is one of two kinds:

- **Talking-head shot.** The avatar faces the camera and speaks. One credit per second.
- **Cinematic motion shot.** The avatar moves through a scene from a text prompt. Two credits per second.

For each shot that starts a new setting, pick a scene: click the scene slot and choose one from your saved scenes, or let the producer suggest one and click **Generate This Scene**. Chained shots continue the previous scene. If you want help writing the motion prompt, click **Direct This Shot** or **Improve My Prompt**.

i

On Starter, videos are capped at 45 seconds and use the talking-head model. Cinematic motion shots and longer videos start on Creator.

Click **Generate**. A confirmation shows the shot count, the total seconds, the credits it uses and what stays in your balance: “Start the render?” Click **Generate · N cr** to start.

## Step 5: It is in the oven

The render page lays out what happens next:

1. **Close the tab.** Rendering keeps going in the background.
2. **We ping you.** You get an email and an in-app notification the moment it is done.
3. **Find it in Videos.** The finished reel lands in your **Videos** tab, ready to download or post.

Rendering takes a few minutes. Open the video to download it, review the captions, or regenerate a shot. See [Download, captions, QA and regenerate](https://avatarfactory.io/help/download-captions-qa-and-regenerate).

## If the render fails

The render page shows “Render failed.” with the reason. Most failures are temporary and a second run works. If it happens twice, write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with the video ID from the Videos page and roughly when you started it. [Rendering failed](https://avatarfactory.io/help/rendering-failed) covers every message.

## Frequently asked questions

Why does the app say to upgrade before I can make a video? +

The trial builds your avatar and the plan renders your video. If you see the message about upgrading to a paid plan to create scenes and videos, your account is still on the trial. Open Billing, start your plan, and the same wizard renders your video with the plan's monthly credits.

How many credits does one video use? +

It depends on the length and the shot types. A talking-head shot costs one credit per second of video, and a cinematic motion shot costs two credits per second. A 30-second talking-head reel is about 30 credits. The wizard shows the exact number before you confirm the render.
