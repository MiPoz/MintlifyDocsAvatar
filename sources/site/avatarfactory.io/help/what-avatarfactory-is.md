# What AvatarFactory is and what you can make with it

AvatarFactory builds AI avatars with a face, a voice and a story, then turns your scripts into ready-to-post reels. Here is what you can make.

Getting started · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 3 min read

## The short version

AvatarFactory makes AI influencers. You describe a character, or pick one from a template. The app builds a face for it, gives it a voice and a backstory, then turns your scripts into finished reels that character speaks. The sign-in page sums it up: build a channel without showing your face.

You do not film, record or edit anything. You write (or generate) the script, pick the shots, and the video renders in the background.

Want to see it before you sign in? The page on how to [build an AI influencer](https://avatarfactory.io/ai-influencer-generator) shows the same flow with finished examples.

## An avatar: face, voice, story

An avatar is the character everything else is built on. It has four parts, and the avatar wizard walks you through them in order:

1. **Identity.** A description of who they are (age, gender, country, look, vibe), optional reference images, and a category.
2. **Story.** A backstory and a target audience. The AI can draft both for you.
3. **Face.** Three generated face candidates. You pick one, refine it, or generate more.
4. **Voice.** A voice description, three audio previews, and one pick.

You can also skip the design work and **Import your avatar**: upload the exact photo you already use and bring your own voice.

Every avatar gets a **Reference Pack** after saving, a set of angles of the same face that keeps the character consistent across scenes and videos.

## Videos: script to reel

The video wizard has four steps: Avatar, Source, Script and Shots. The Source step is where the script comes from. You have four options: an AI content expert that brainstorms hooks, a reel or TikTok or YouTube link that gets transcribed, a reel from an account you follow, or writing from scratch.

Each shot is either a talking-head shot (the avatar faces the camera) or a cinematic motion shot (the avatar moves through a scene). Rendering runs in the background. Close the tab if you want. The finished video lands in your **Videos** tab with an email and an in-app notification.

## Scenes and images

Scenes are the still shots your videos are built from: a penthouse at dusk, a trading desk at night, a yacht deck. You describe one, clone one from a reference image, pick one from the templates gallery, or upload your own. Saved scenes live in the avatar’s library and are ready to use in any shot.

## Growth tools

On the plans that include them, you can follow accounts in your niche, see what is going viral before it cools, and rebuild a working reel’s structure with your own avatar in it. These live under **Followed accounts**, **Viral Radar** and **Reel Analytics**.
