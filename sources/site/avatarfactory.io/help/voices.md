# Voices: pick one, regenerate, or connect your ElevenLabs account

Every video speaks in the avatar's voice. Design one from a description, regenerate a preview, change it later, or bring your own ElevenLabs voice.

Avatars · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 4 min read

## Where voices live

An avatar gets its voice in one of three places:

- **The avatar wizard**, Voice step, when you create an avatar.
- **The import wizard**, Voice step, when you bring your own face.
- **The avatar page**, through **Edit voice**, any time later.

If you try to make a video with an avatar that has no voice, the app stops on a page that says the avatar needs a voice. Click **Generate Voice** and you land on the Voice step; it takes about 30 seconds.

The voice you pick here is the one that speaks every script. For [how the voice reaches the video](https://avatarfactory.io/ai-avatar-generator), that page shows the finished result.

## Design a voice from a description

1. Start with the **Voice description**. It drafts itself from the face, story and references; click **Suggest with AI** if it is empty, or **Regenerate prompt** for another draft. It needs at least 10 characters.
2. Make it specific. The tips popover lists what matters: gender, origin (“Emirati from Dubai” beats “Middle Eastern”), accent, pacing, pitch, timbre (pick two or three words like warm, gravelly, smooth), and emotion or persona.
3. Optional: **Advanced · tuning & preview text** has **Loudness**, **Guidance**, and a box for a custom line the previews will say (up to 200 characters).
4. Click **Generate 3 voice previews**. One credit for the batch of three.
5. Press play on **Voice 1**, **Voice 2** and **Voice 3**. Click **Use this voice** on the one that sounds like a real person without trying. It reads **✓ Picked**.
6. Click **Finish avatar →** (or **Save avatar →** in the import wizard).

Voice carries a lot of the realism. If none of the three convince you, do not settle: regenerate.

## Regenerate a preview

- **One tile.** Under the preview, type what should change (“warmer, slower, less announcer”) and click **Regenerate**. A third of a credit.
- **A fresh batch.** Click **Generate 3 more**. One credit. The new three replace the grid, so pick before you regenerate if one was close.

If you see “Pick one of the three voices first.”, play the previews, click **Use this voice** on one, then try that action again.

## Change the voice later

1. Open the avatar from **Avatars** and click **Edit voice**.
2. The Voice step opens with your current voice sample at the top.
3. Generate new previews and click **Use this voice** on one, or switch to your ElevenLabs list.
4. Click **Finish avatar →** to save the change.

## Use a voice from your ElevenLabs account

If you already run a voice in ElevenLabs, connect your account and pick it. Speech for that avatar runs on your ElevenLabs credits.

### Connect the account

1. Open **Settings**, scroll to **Connected Accounts**, and next to **ElevenLabs** click **Connect**. (The import wizard’s **Use my ElevenLabs voice** panel has the same form.)
2. In ElevenLabs, click **Create API Key** and name it.
3. Give the key these permissions: enable **Text to Speech** and **Speech to Speech**, set **Voices** to **Read**, and set **User** to **Access**.
4. Copy the key (it starts with sk\_) and paste it in the box. Press Enter or click the connect button.

If you see “That key didn’t verify. Check it and try again.”, the key lost a character in the paste, or it is missing one of the permissions. Create a new key with the four permissions above and paste it again.

### Pick the voice

1. On the Voice step, choose **Use my ElevenLabs voice**.
2. Search with **Search your voices** and click the row. Each row has a play button to preview it.
3. Save the avatar.

If the list says “No voices in this ElevenLabs account yet. Create one at elevenlabs.io, or design a new voice here.”, the connected account has no voices. Create one there, or switch to **✦ Design a new voice**.

### Reconnect or disconnect

In **Settings**, the ElevenLabs row shows **Connected** with your account label, or **Not connected**. If a stored key stops working, the row offers **Reconnect**: paste a fresh key. **Disconnect** removes the key; the confirmation spells out what that costs: “Avatars using these voices will need a reconnect before their next video.”

> **Warning:** Canceling your subscription releases the voices designed in AvatarFactory, and they cannot be recovered. If you plan to come back, pause instead. See [Cancel your subscription](https://avatarfactory.io/help/cancel-your-subscription).

## Frequently asked questions

How much does a voice cost in credits? +

One credit for a batch of three previews, and a third of a credit to regenerate a single preview with a note. Saving the avatar with the chosen voice costs nothing extra. Speech in your videos is included in the per-second video rate, so you are not charged again for the voice when you render.

Can I change the voice after the avatar is saved? +

Yes. Open the avatar page and click Edit voice. It reopens the Voice step with your current voice shown, and you can generate three new previews and pick one, or switch to a voice from your connected ElevenLabs account. Videos you already rendered keep the voice they were made with.
