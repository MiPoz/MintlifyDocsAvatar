# Rendering failed: every message and what to do

Each render failure message the app shows, what it means, the fix for each one, and how to resume a failed video without redoing the work.

Videos · Last reviewed September 23, 2026 · 6 min read

## Where the message appears

A failed render shows up in three places, always in plain words and never as a raw error code:

- On the **Videos** page, the card gets a **Failed** badge and its meta line shows the first sentence of the message.
- In the in-app notifications panel, the full message.
- On the oven screen, if you were still watching: “Render failed.” followed by the reason.

The video page itself shows the **Failed** badge and the **Resume & Re-render →** button. Every message below names its fix. Apply it, then resume the video.

## Rendering failed. Try again, and contact support if it keeps happening.

The general message. Something stopped mid-render and there is no specific cause you can act on.

1. Open the video and click **Resume & Re-render →**.
2. On the Shots step, click **Generate · N cr** again.
3. If it fails a second time, write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with the video ID (it is in the page address, after /videos/) and the time you started the render. We check the job on our side and reply within one business day.

## Rendering stopped: you ran out of credits. Top up, then run it again.

The render needed more credits than your balance held. Your credits are the monthly allotment of your plan; they refresh every billing period.

1. Open your account menu at the top of the app, click **Billing**, and check your balance and your renewal date.
2. To render now, upgrade to the next plan tier, which adds credits right away. Otherwise wait for the refresh.
3. Shorten the cut if you want it to fit the credits you have: on the Shots step, cinematic shots cost 2 credits per second and talking-head shots 1 credit per second.
4. Click **Resume & Re-render →** and generate again.

## Rendering failed: this avatar has no voice yet. Add a voice, then try again.

The avatar on this video has no voice, so there is nothing to speak the script.

1. Open **Avatars**, open the avatar, and add or design a voice. [Voices](https://avatarfactory.io/help/voices) walks through it.
2. Return to the video and click **Resume & Re-render →**.

i

The wizard normally stops this before the render: an avatar without a voice cannot be picked on the avatar step. You see this message when the voice was removed after the draft was made.

## Rendering failed: one of the images was rejected. Swap it out, then try again.

A scene image or a reference image was refused by the video service.

1. Click **Resume & Re-render →**.
2. On the shot that uses the rejected image, pick a different saved scene or generate a new one from the scene drawer.
3. Generate again. If you cannot tell which image it was, swap the scene on the first cinematic shot first; those are the shots that start from an image.

## Rendering stopped: the script or a shot tripped the content filter. Edit it, then try again.

The words or the direction of a shot hit the service’s content filter.

1. Click **Resume & Re-render →**, then go back to the Script step.
2. Rewrite the passage that could read as violent, sexual, hateful, or as a real person’s likeness. Direction text on the shot cards counts too.
3. Return to Shots and generate again.

[Content rules, likeness and copyright](https://avatarfactory.io/help/content-rules-likeness-and-copyright) lists the rules that apply to every video.

## Rendering failed: the video service did not respond. Try again in a minute.

A temporary outage or timeout on the rendering side. Nothing is wrong with your draft.

1. Wait a few minutes.
2. Click **Resume & Re-render →** and generate again.
3. If it happens three times in a row, write to us with the video ID; we watch for service problems and can tell you when they clear.

## Render failed. (on the oven screen)

The oven screen adds the reason after “Render failed.” When it reads “Render failed for an unknown reason” or “Failed to start render”, the render did not get far enough to store a cause. Treat it as the general message above: resume, retry once, then write to us.

## The render did not start. Try again.

Shown on the Studio step (podcast) and the talking-window step (split-window) when the render request itself was refused. If the line starts with “Fix before rendering:”, the template found script rules that block the render. Click **Edit the script →** or use the co-writer’s fix option, then click **Animate →** again.

## This take didn’t render. The credits were returned, fix and try again anytime.

This is a single shot, not the whole video. The rest of the video is intact.

1. On the video page, find the shot card with the warning.
2. Click **↻ Try Again** on that card, or **Fix This Shot** to change the transcript, the direction or the scene first.
3. When the new take is ready, click **Review Takes**, keep the one you want, then click **Rebuild Final Video** in the bar at the bottom.

## The copy stopped while preparing the avatar’s reference pack

Shown on a **Copy a reel** video. A copy draws each person from their avatar’s Reference Pack, so when a cast avatar has no pack yet, the copy generates one first and waits for it (the card reads “Preparing the Avatar’s Reference Pack”). The copy stops only when the pack cannot be made:

- **”…‘s reference pack could not be generated.”** Open that avatar, go to its **Reference Pack** tab and generate the pack there, then start the copy again.
- **”…‘s reference pack was not ready after 20 minutes.”** The pack took too long. Open the avatar’s **Reference Pack** tab: when the pack shows as ready, start the copy again.
- **“You’re out of credits. Upgrade your plan to get more.”** The pack costs the same credits as the one made when an avatar is saved, and there were not enough for it. Nothing was spent on the video. Top up or upgrade, then start the copy again.

## Resume a failed video

Every failed video keeps its draft. **Resume & Re-render →** on the video page reopens the wizard with the same avatar, script, shots, scene picks and caption settings, and lands on the last build step. Fix what failed, then generate again. Nothing is redone from scratch.

If the button is missing, the video was created outside the wizard or the draft was deleted; use **Generate Similar →** instead, which rebuilds the shot list from the video.

## What happens to your credits

A render that fails returns its credits. A shot that fails on its own says “The credits were returned” on its card. The confirmation before every render shows what the render costs and what stays in your balance, so you can compare before and after. [Credits and failed renders](https://avatarfactory.io/help/credits-and-failed-renders) covers the details.

## When to write to us

Write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) when the same message comes back after one retry, when the credits did not come back within a few minutes, or when a render sits on a stage far past the 10 to 20 minute range. Include the video ID, the time you started, and a screenshot of the card. We reply within one business day.

## Frequently asked questions

Do I get my credits back when a render fails? +

Yes. A render that fails returns its credits to your balance, and a shot that fails on its own says so on its card: the credits were returned. You are only charged for a video that completes. If your balance does not reflect this within a few minutes, write to Support@AvatarFactory.IO with the video ID.

Do I have to rebuild the whole video after a failure? +

No. Open the failed video and click Resume & Re-render. The wizard rebuilds itself from the saved draft, with the script, the shots and the scenes exactly as you left them, and lands on the Shots step so you can fix what failed and render again.
