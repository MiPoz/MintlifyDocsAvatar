# Upgrade, downgrade, and scheduled plan changes

Upgrades apply now and add credits instantly. Downgrades wait for the end of your paid term. How to switch, read the summary, and cancel a scheduled change.

Billing and account · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 4 min read

## Two kinds of change

Every plan switch is either an upgrade or a downgrade, and the app treats them differently:

- **An upgrade applies now.** Moving to a higher plan, or from monthly to annual on the same plan. You are charged the price difference today, the features unlock immediately, and the credit difference is added to your balance right away. Your renewal date does not move.
- **A downgrade is scheduled.** Moving to a lower plan, or from annual to monthly on the same plan. Nothing is charged today. You keep your current plan and credits until the end of the term you paid for, then the new plan starts.

The pricing page lists [what each plan costs](https://avatarfactory.io/pricing) on both billing intervals, if you want to compare before you switch.

## Upgrade to a higher plan

1. Open **Plan & Usage** from the sidebar.
2. Under **Other plans**, find the plan you want. On the Influencer card, pick the credit tier from the **Monthly credits** dropdown first.
3. Click **Switch**.
4. A summary titled **Confirm Your Switch** opens. Check the four lines: **Plan** (the plan and whether it is billed monthly or annually), **Charged now** (the price difference), **Credits added now** (the extra credits you get on confirm), and **Payment method** (the card on file, for example VISA ending 4242).
5. To pay with a different card, click **Change payment method**, enter the card, and click **Save Card**.
6. Click **Confirm**.

The charge is the full price difference between the two plans, not a prorated slice. In return, the full credit difference lands in your balance the moment you confirm. Example: Sarah Johnson is on Starter (325 credits) and switches to Creator (790 credits). She is charged $60 now and 465 credits are added immediately.

If the card is declined, the summary shows the error inline and nothing changes: you stay on your current plan, no credits are added, and you can fix the card and try again.

## Switch from monthly to annual

On the Plan and Usage page, the current plan card has a **Switch to Annual · Save 20%** button. It opens the same summary. Because annual is treated as an upgrade, the switch applies now and you are charged the difference today. From then on you are billed once a year. Credits still arrive month by month.

## Downgrade to a lower plan

1. Open **Plan & Usage**.
2. Under **Other plans**, click **Switch** on the lower plan (or pick a lower Influencer tier from the dropdown).
3. A summary titled **Schedule Plan Change** opens. It shows **Due today: $0** and **Starts on** with the date your current term ends.
4. Click **Schedule Downgrade**.

A banner appears at the top of the page: “Scheduled change: switching to on . You keep your current plan until then.”

Switching from annual to monthly on the same plan works the same way: it is scheduled for the end of the annual term you already paid for.

## Cancel a scheduled downgrade

Changed your mind before the effective date?

1. Open **Plan & Usage**.
2. On the scheduled change banner, click **Cancel Downgrade**.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

Your current plan simply continues. Choosing any other plan while a downgrade is scheduled also replaces the scheduled change with the new decision.

## What does not change

- **Your renewal date** stays the same through an upgrade.
- **Your card** stays on file unless you change it in the summary.
- **Your avatars, videos and settings** are untouched by any plan change. If you downgrade below the number of active avatars your new plan allows, the extra ones are not deleted; you will be asked to archive one before you can activate another.

i

A plan change is not a cancellation. To stop the subscription, see [Cancel your subscription](https://avatarfactory.io/help/cancel-your-subscription). To take a break without losing anything, [Pause your plan for 30 days](https://avatarfactory.io/help/pause-your-plan) covers the free pause.

## If something goes wrong

“Could not load the summary. Please close and try again.” means the price preview did not come back. Close the summary and click **Switch** again. If it repeats, or a charge appears on your card without the plan changing, write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with the email on your account and the time you tried. We check the charge on our side before you try again.

## Frequently asked questions

When does an upgrade take effect? +

Immediately. When you confirm an upgrade, the price difference between your current plan and the new one is charged to the card on file, the new plan's features unlock at once, and the credit difference is added to your balance on the spot. Your renewal date stays exactly where it was.

When does a downgrade take effect? +

At the end of the term you have already paid for. Nothing is charged today. You keep your current plan and its credits until the effective date shown on the Plan and Usage page, then the new plan starts. Until that date you can cancel the scheduled change with one click and stay where you are.
