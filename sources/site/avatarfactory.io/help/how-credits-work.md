# How credits work: what each image, voice and second of video costs

Every image, voice and second of video costs credits. Here is the price of each action, when credits refill, and where to see your balance.

Credits and plans · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 4 min read

## Credits are the one unit for everything you make

Every plan comes with a monthly pool of credits. Each action that generates something spends a fixed number of credits from that pool. There is one pool per account, it refills every billing month, and the Plan and Usage page always shows how much is left.

The price of each action is the same on every plan. What changes between plans is how many credits you get each month and which features are unlocked.

## What each action costs

| Action | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| One generated image (a face, a scene, a background) | 1 |
| Refining or rerolling one image | 1 |
| Saving an avatar (the automatic angle pack) | 6, charged once at save |
| Designing a voice (a batch of 3 candidates) | 1 |
| Regenerating a single voice | 0.33 |
| Video on the standard model | 1 per second |
| Video on the cinematic model | 2 per second |

The voice track inside a video is included in the per-second rate. You are not charged separately for the speech in a video.

Script writing with the co-writer never spends credits. It has its own daily cap of 50 turns on a paid plan, and the count resets each day.

## A worked example

Sarah Johnson is on Creator, which includes 790 credits a month. In one session she:

1. Generates 4 scene images: 4 credits.
2. Refines one of them twice: 2 credits.
3. Renders a 40-second video on the standard model: 40 credits.
4. Renders a 20-second video with a 5-second cinematic hook and a 15-second standard body: 10 plus 15, so 25 credits.

That session cost 71 credits and left her with 719 for the rest of the month.

## How many videos is that?

The plan cards use one estimate: your monthly credits divided by a 45-second video on the standard model. That works out to about 7 videos a month on Starter, about 17 on Creator, and about 33 on the base Influencer tier. Shorter videos stretch further, and cinematic shots cost double, so treat the estimate as a starting point rather than a quota.

## When credits refill

Credits refill at the start of each billing month. Unused credits do not roll over.

i

On an annual plan you pay once a year, but the credits still arrive month by month. Your yearly total is not dropped into your account all at once.

The Plan and Usage page shows two lines under **Credits**: **Monthly credits remaining**, written as remaining out of included, and **Next refill**, the date your next monthly allocation lands. If you are not signed in yet, [the plans and their monthly credits](https://avatarfactory.io/pricing) are listed on the pricing page.

## Reserved, settled, released

When you start a generation, the credits it needs are held first, then charged when the work finishes. The **Credit Usage** list on the Plan and Usage page shows each step with a status:

- **reserved**: held while your generation runs. Set aside, not yet charged.
- **settled**: the final charge for a completed generation. For video this is reconciled to the real rendered length, so it can be a little less or a little more than the amount first reserved.
- **released**: the hold went back to your balance in full because the generation failed or was canceled.
- **expired**: a leftover hold from a generation that never finished, returned to your balance automatically.

A failed render is never charged. [Credits and failed renders](https://avatarfactory.io/help/credits-and-failed-renders) has the details.

## Where to see your balance

1. Open **Plan & Usage** from the sidebar.
2. Read **Monthly credits remaining** on the **Credits** card.
3. Scroll to **Credit Usage** for the line-by-line history, newest first, with the balance left after each charge.

If a number on that page does not match what you did, write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with the email on your account and the date of the charge, and we will check the ledger on our side.

## Frequently asked questions

How many credits does one video cost? +

A video costs credits per second of finished footage, and the rate depends on the model. The standard model charges 1 credit per second, and the cinematic model charges 2 credits per second. A 30-second video on the standard model costs 30 credits. The voice track is included in that rate, so there is no separate charge for speech.

Do unused credits roll over to next month? +

No. Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation that resets at the start of the next billing month, and unused subscription credits do not roll over. This is true on annual plans too: you pay once a year, but the credits arrive month by month. Your Plan and Usage page shows the next refill date.
