# Import your own face: file rules and what makes a good photo

Bring an avatar you already have. The import wizard uses your photo exactly as uploaded: file rules, extra references, the story step, and voice options.

Avatars · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 3 min read

## When to import instead of generate

You already have a character with a face people recognize, and you want that exact face in AvatarFactory. Importing keeps it pixel for pixel. If you only have a mood or a look in mind, generate instead: [Create an avatar from scratch](https://avatarfactory.io/help/create-an-avatar-from-scratch).

Importing your own photo is also how you [clone yourself with AI](https://avatarfactory.io/clone-yourself-with-ai), so the avatar on camera is you.

## Open the import wizard

1. Open **Avatars** and click **\+ New Avatar**.
2. On **How do you want to build it?**, click **Import your avatar** (“Already have one? Upload its image and bring your own ElevenLabs voice”).
3. The wizard has three steps: Upload, Story, Voice.

## Step 1: Upload

The heading asks **Who are we importing?**

1. Type the **Avatar name** (for example, Sarah Johnson).
2. Under **Avatar images**, add the face. Drop a file on the card or click to pick one. The first image becomes the face and is used exactly as uploaded.
3. Optional: add up to five more images of the same person, by file or by pasting a link. These become extra references in the avatar’s Reference Pack. The link field only works once the face is in place.
4. Click **Continue →**. Name and image are required.

Under the grid you see the file name, its dimensions and its size, plus a note: we build the full reference pack from this image after you save.

### File rules

- **Format:** PNG or JPG. WebP and iPhone HEIC photos are accepted too.
- **Size:** up to 15 MB per file.
- **Count:** six images in total, one face plus five references.

If a file is rejected, the message says why: ” is not an image we can use. Upload a PNG or JPG portrait instead.” or ” is 18.2 MB. The limit is 15 MB.” Fix the file and add it again.

### What makes a good photo

- A clear, front-facing portrait, eyes open, face filling a good part of the frame.
- Even lighting, no heavy shadows across the face, no sunglasses or hands in front of the mouth.
- One person in the picture. A group shot confuses the reference pack.
- Sharp and unfiltered. Beauty filters and heavy compression carry into every scene.
- Upright. Rotate the photo before uploading.

For the extra references, add different angles and expressions of the same person. They give the scene generator a better likeness to work from.

## Step 2: Story

Same as the generation wizard: a **Backstory** (click **Ideate for me** for three angles) and a **Target audience** (click **Generate** for a draft). Nothing is charged on this step. Click **Continue to voice →**.

## Step 3: Voice

Three options in the segmented control:

- **Use my ElevenLabs voice.** Paste your ElevenLabs API key, then search and pick a voice from your account. Speech runs on your ElevenLabs credits.
- **✦ Design a new voice.** The same three-preview audition as the generation wizard.
- **Skip for now.** Save without a voice and add one later from the avatar page.

Click **Save avatar →**. All three paths are explained in [Voices](https://avatarfactory.io/help/voices).

i

Voice is optional here, but every video uses the avatar’s voice. Before the first video, the app asks you to add one.

## After saving

The avatar opens on its page with your photo as the face. The Reference Pack builds in the background from that image. From here you can generate scenes, which place that exact face in new settings: [Scenes and images](https://avatarfactory.io/help/scenes-and-images).

## Frequently asked questions

Will the app change my photo? +

No. The first image you add is the face, and it is used exactly as uploaded. The app does not regenerate, restyle or crop it. After you save, it builds the reference pack of angles from that image, and any extra references you add help the scene generator match the likeness in new settings.

Can I import a real person? +

Only a face you have the right to use: your own, or one you created and own. Importing someone else's likeness without permission is against the content rules and gets the avatar removed. If you are unsure, read the content rules article before you upload, or write to Support@AvatarFactory.IO.
