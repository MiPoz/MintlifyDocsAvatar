# Your first avatar, the fastest path

The fastest path from a fresh account to a saved avatar with a face and a voice: pick a template, keep the defaults, choose a face, pick a voice, finish.

Getting started · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 3 min read

## What you will have at the end

One saved avatar with a name, a face, a voice and a backstory, ready for its first video. The fastest route is a template with the defaults left alone. You can edit any field later from the avatar page.

## Step 1: Open the avatar builder

1. On **Getting Started**, click **Create Avatar**. Or open **Avatars** in the sidebar and click **\+ New Avatar**.
2. You land on **How do you want to build it?** with three paths: **Start from scratch**, **Import your avatar**, and a row of **Featured templates** in your niche.
3. Click a featured template you like. (Want to see them all? Click **See all templates** and use the search and the niche tabs.)

## Step 2: Identity (keep the defaults)

The Identity step opens as **Customize your template**. Every field is pre-filled: the description, the reference images, the category. For a first avatar, read it once and click **Continue to story →**. You can change anything, but you do not have to.

## Step 3: Story (let the AI write it)

1. Under **Backstory**, click **Ideate for me**. Three angles appear. Click **Use this →** on the one you like.
2. Under **Target audience**, click **Generate** and pick one of the options.
3. Click **Continue to face previews →**.

i

This click starts the face generation, and that is where credits are used for the first time: one credit per face, three faces.

## Step 4: Face (pick one)

Three face tiles render one by one, with a countdown that starts around “~75s left”. While you wait, name the avatar in the **Name** field on this step, or click **Brainstorm 5 names** and pick one.

1. When the tiles land, click **Choose** on the face you like. It shows **✓ Picked**.
2. Not quite right? Click **Refine** on a tile, pick a chip like **Hair** or **Expression**, type what to change (“Straighter hair, slightly older, add thin glasses”), and click **Apply to This Face**. The refined face is added as a new card next to the original, so nothing is lost. Each refine is one credit.
3. Want a fresh set? Click **Generate 3 more**. It confirms the cost (three credits) and starts a new set you can switch between.
4. Click **Continue to voice →**.

## Step 5: Voice (three previews, one pick)

1. The **Voice description** drafts itself from your face, story and references. If it is empty, click **Suggest with AI**.
2. Click **Generate 3 voice previews**. One credit for the three.
3. Press play on each tile. Click **Use this voice** on the one that sounds like a real person without trying.
4. Click **Finish avatar →**.

If you want to move on and pick a voice later, click **Save without voice**. You will be asked to generate one before the first video.

## Step 6: Done

The done screen confirms the avatar is saved and offers **Make your first video**. Saving also builds the **Reference Pack**, six angles of the chosen face, and charges six credits for it.

Your avatar now lives on the **Avatars** page. Open it to edit the backstory, add signature phrases, generate scenes, or change the voice.

## Frequently asked questions

How many credits does the first avatar use? +

About ten. The three face candidates are one credit each, the three voice previews are one credit together, and saving the avatar charges six credits for the reference pack of angles. Refining a face or regenerating a voice adds a little more. The trial's 15 credits cover one full build.

Can I come back later and finish? +

Yes. The wizard saves a draft as you go. Leave with Save and exit in the top bar, and the next time you open New Avatar you will see a Continue a Draft prompt. Drafts also appear under the Drafts tab on the Avatars page, ready to resume where you stopped.
