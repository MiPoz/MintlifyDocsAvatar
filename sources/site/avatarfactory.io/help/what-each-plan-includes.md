# What your plan includes: limits, models and growth tools

Starter, Creator and Influencer side by side: monthly credits, active avatars, video models, video length, Viral Radar and the growth tools on each one.

Credits and plans · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 4 min read

## The three plans at a glance

AvatarFactory has three plans: Starter, Creator and Influencer. Every plan starts with the same 1 dollar three-day trial, which comes with 15 credits. Prices below are in US dollars.

| | Starter | Creator | Influencer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly price | $39 | $99 | $249 |
| Annual price | $372 a year ($31 a month) | $948 a year ($79 a month) | $2,388 a year ($199 a month) |
| Monthly credits | 325 | 790 | 1,490, with higher tiers |
| Active avatars | 1 | 3 | Unlimited |
| Video models | Standard only | All | All |
| Video length | Up to 45 seconds | No cap | No cap |
| Followed accounts | No | Yes | Yes |
| Reel analytics | No | Yes | Yes |
| Viral Radar | Not included | 10 | 30 and up by tier |

The annual price is charged once a year and works out to about 20 percent less than paying monthly. Credits on an annual plan still arrive month by month.

## Starter, $39 a month

Built for one avatar and one channel. The plan card lists 1 avatar, 1 social account and 1K resolution images. You get 325 credits a month, which the app estimates as about 7 videos a month at 45 seconds on the standard model.

Two limits to know before you pick it:

- **One video model.** Starter renders on the standard model only. The cinematic engine is not available.
- **45 seconds per video.** The video wizard shows a meter such as ”39s of 45s” under the label “Video length your plan renders”. Go over and the label changes to “Over the length your plan renders”, with a **Shorten to Fit** button that trims the longest shots down to the cap. You can undo the trim.

## Creator, $99 a month

The plan most creators end up on. The card lists 3 avatars, 3 social accounts, HD images and Auto-publish ([what is live today](https://avatarfactory.io/help/posts-auto-publishing-and-campaigns)). You get 790 credits a month, about 17 videos a month at 45 seconds on the standard model, and every video model with no length cap.

Creator also unlocks the growth tools: followed accounts, reel analytics, and Viral Radar with a capacity of 10.

## Influencer, from $249 a month

Unlimited active avatars, HD images, multi-account publishing, API access and priority support. Every video model, no length cap, followed accounts and reel analytics.

Influencer is one card with a **Monthly credits** dropdown. Each option is its own tier with its own price and Viral Radar capacity:

| Monthly credits | Monthly price | Viral Radar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,490 | $249 | 30 |
| 2,490 | $249 | 50 |
| 3,990 | $399 | 70 |
| 5,990 | $599 | 90 |

The 1,490 and 2,490 options both show $249 a month in the app today. The dropdown prints each tier’s own price next to its credits, and the switch summary shows what you are charged before you confirm, so read those two before you pick a tier.

The app’s video estimate for these tiers, at 45 seconds on the standard model, is about 33, 55, 88 and 133 videos a month.

## What the numbers mean

- **Active avatars** counts avatars in use. On Starter and Creator, when every slot is full the app shows “You’ve used every active avatar slot on your plan” and offers two ways forward: upgrade, or archive an avatar you no longer use to free a slot.
- **Video models.** The standard model charges 1 credit per second, the cinematic model 2 credits per second. Only Creator and Influencer can use the cinematic model.
- **Viral Radar** is the number of accounts you can track and the daily scan budget. Each step up the ladder adds 20.
- **Credits** are the same unit on every plan. [How credits work](https://avatarfactory.io/help/how-credits-work) lists what each action costs.

## Picking a plan

1. If you are testing one avatar on one channel and short talking-head videos are enough, start on **Starter**.
2. If you want the cinematic model, videos longer than 45 seconds, or the growth tools, pick **Creator**.
3. If you run more than three avatars, publish to several accounts, or need API access, pick **Influencer** and choose the credit tier that matches your monthly volume.

You can move between plans at any time from the Plan and Usage page. Upgrades apply immediately and add the credit difference right away; downgrades are scheduled for the end of the period you already paid for. [Upgrade, downgrade, and scheduled plan changes](https://avatarfactory.io/help/upgrade-downgrade-and-scheduled-changes) walks through both.

## Frequently asked questions

Which plan do I need for the cinematic video model? +

The cinematic model comes with Creator and up. Starter renders on the standard model only, which charges 1 credit per second and caps a video at 45 seconds. Creator and Influencer unlock every model with no length cap, so a shot can use the cinematic engine at 2 credits per second whenever the script calls for it.

What is the difference between the Influencer credit tiers? +

Every Influencer tier has unlimited active avatars, every video model, no length cap and the growth tools. The tiers differ in monthly credits and Viral Radar capacity. You pick the tier from the Monthly credits dropdown on the Influencer card, and you can move between tiers later from the Plan and Usage page.
