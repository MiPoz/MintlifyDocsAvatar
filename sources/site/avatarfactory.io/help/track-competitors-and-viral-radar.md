# Tracked Competitors and Viral Radar in your account

Add Instagram and TikTok handles to Tracked Competitors, read growth and indexed reels, rescan inside your daily Viral Radar budget, and open one account.

Growth tools · Last reviewed September 17, 2026 · 4 min read

## What Viral Radar is

**Viral Radar** is the sidebar section with two pages: **Viral Accounts** (the Tracked Competitors page) and **Viral Reels** (Reel Analytics). This article covers accounts. [Reel analytics and copied reels](https://avatarfactory.io/help/reel-analytics-and-copied-reels) covers reels.

Tracking an account does three things, and the page lists all three. It pulls the account’s last 50 reels and indexes every hook, format and hashtag. It rescans on a schedule and tracks 7-day velocity and follower growth. And it surfaces the reels that are climbing, so you can copy the pattern.

Tracking needs Creator or higher. On Starter the page opens on an upgrade prompt.

For what to do with the accounts once they are in, read the guide on [tracking competitor reels](https://avatarfactory.io/guides/track-competitor-instagram-reels).

## Track a new account

1. Open **Viral Accounts** and click **\+ Track New Account**. On an empty page, type into the field and click **Track**.
2. Pick **Instagram** or **TikTok**.
3. Type the username or paste the profile URL and confirm. A handle uses letters, numbers, dots or underscores, up to 40 characters; anything else is refused before it is sent.

The first scan runs in the background. The account appears with “Last scan” filling in as the reels index. An account that stops posting is scanned less often (every 3 days after a week without a post, weekly after two weeks, monthly after a month) and the row tells you so with a “Quiet · checked weekly” marker in the Last scan column, worded for that account’s schedule. The next scan that finds a new reel puts it straight back on the fast schedule.

## Your Viral Radar limit

One number governs both how many accounts you can track and how many scans you can run per day, whichever runs out first:

| Plan | Accounts and daily scans |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | Not included |
| Creator | 10 |
| Influencer | 30 |
| Influencer 2,490 / 3,990 / 5,990 credit tiers | 50 / 70 / 90 |

The **Rescan** button shows the day’s remaining scans as a counter such as “30/30”, with “N of 30 scans left today” on hover. When it reads 0, the tooltip says “No scans left today, resets tomorrow.”

Before you track your first account, the empty page reads “Track up to 50 Instagram or TikTok accounts in your niche.” That line describes the feature, not your own allowance. The number that applies to you is the one in the table above. When you reach it, the app says so with your own numbers in it: “You’re tracking 10 of 10 Viral Radar accounts. Upgrade to add more.”

## Read the page

The header tabs split the list: **All**, **My Follows** (accounts you added), **✦ Verified** (accounts we curate and refresh ourselves, read-only for you) and **☆ Favorites** (accounts you starred). Below, a search box, a **Category** filter, **Save as default** for your filter setup, and a sort control. Every account you track is given a category automatically on its first scan, and an account we cannot place shows as **Other** until a later scan reads it better. You can change any category yourself on your own accounts, and your choice stays.

**▦ Table** is the default view on a laptop, with sortable columns for 7-Day Growth, Followers, Reels Indexed and Last Scan. **▤ Cards** shows the same accounts as tiles; phones always use cards. Each tile shows the handle, bio, Followers, Reels indexed, 7-day growth, and **View reels →**.

## Rescan and archive

- **Rescan** in the header opens a dialog to pick which of your follows to refresh. The caption under the button sets the limit: “Rescan covers the accounts you follow. Verified accounts refresh automatically.” Each scan counts against the daily budget.
- Each of your own accounts also has a **Rescan** and an **Archive** button. Archive removes the account from your list.

i

Verified accounts cannot be rescanned or archived by you; they are refreshed on our schedule.

## Drill into one account

Click **View reels →** on an account (or the handle on Reel Analytics). The account page shows:

- A tracking strip: tracking since, reels indexed, last scanned, and scan count.
- Four KPI tiles that switch the chart: **Followers**, **Total Views**, **Avg Views / Reel** and **Avg Engagement**, over **7 days**, **30 days**, **3 months** or **All time**. The chart needs at least two data points, so a freshly tracked account shows “Not enough data to draw a chart yet.” until the next scan.
- The account’s reels, top views first, with the same filters, sort and **⧉ Copy Reel** action as Reel Analytics.

**← Reel Analytics** returns to the cross-account view. A **Rescan** button in the header refreshes this one account.

## When a scan fails

An account whose newest scan errored shows a warning on its row with the last attempt time. Click **Rescan** once. If it keeps failing, the account may be private, renamed or removed on the platform. Open the profile from the link on its row and check. If the profile is public and the scan still fails, write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with the handle.
