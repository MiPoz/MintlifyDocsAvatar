# Make a video from a script, a reel, or a template

The complete reference for + New Video: pick a template, an avatar and a script source, build the shots, then send the video to render.

Videos · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 4 min read

## Before you start

You need one avatar with a voice. An avatar without a voice cannot render, so its tile in the wizard sends you to add a voice first. The Starter plan renders talking-head shots only and caps a video at 45 seconds; Creator and Influencer lift both limits.

For what comes out the other end, the [script to captioned video](https://avatarfactory.io/ai-avatar-generator) page shows finished examples of both shot types.

## Start a new video

1. Open **Videos** and click **\+ New Video**. If you have no videos yet, the page shows **Make first video →** instead.
2. The wizard opens on the template gallery, “What are we making today?”. Pick a template card, or click **Continue with UGC Reel →** in the footer for the classic talking-to-camera reel. Cards marked **Creator+** need that plan or higher.
3. On the avatar step, click the avatar you want on camera, then **Continue to source →**.

i

If an upgrade screen appears after the avatar step, your current plan does not include video rendering yet. Pick a plan to continue.

## Choose where the script comes from

The Source step asks “Where does the script come from?” and offers four tiles:

1. **Ideate with AI content expert** (marked Recommended). The wizard brainstorms six hooks for your avatar’s niche. Pick one, add a note in “What didn’t land?” if you want a fresh batch, then click **Continue to script →**. A short Writing step turns the hook into a full script before the editor opens.
2. **Paste a Reel / TikTok URL**. Paste an Instagram, TikTok or YouTube link and click **Transcribe**, or **✦ Full Clone · 10 cr** to rebuild the reel shot by shot. Then click **Use this transcript →** or **Review the cloned script →**.
3. **From a followed account**. Browse recent reels from accounts you track and click **Use ▾** on one. This tile is locked on Starter.
4. **Write from scratch**. A blank editor.

## Edit the script

The Script step shows the source card, the editor, and the Script Co-writer on the right. Under the editor you see the word count and “~N sec at your avatar’s pace”. If the estimate passes 60 seconds, a note asks you to trim before the render.

Useful controls:

- **80/20**, **Rewrite everything** and **Get inspiration only** in the co-writer propose a rewrite. Click **Apply** on the proposal card to use it, or **↺ Revert** to get your original text back.
- The Hear It button plays the script in your avatar’s voice before you spend any credits.
- **Change source** returns to the Source step. **Swap avatar →** returns to the avatar grid.

Click **Continue to Shots →** when the script reads right.

## Build the shots and render

The Shots step turns the script into a shot list. Each shot has a model, a scene and a slice of the script. [The video wizard explained](https://avatarfactory.io/help/the-video-wizard-explained) covers every control. For a first video, keep the producer’s cut, set **Subtitles** to **On** or **Off**, and check the length meter.

1. Click **Generate · N cr**. The number is the credit cost of this render.
2. Read the confirmation, “Start the render?”, which lists the shots, the total seconds, the credits used and what stays in your balance. Click **Generate · N cr** to start or **Not yet** to go back.
3. The oven screen, “Your video is in the oven.”, confirms the render is running. Click **Run in background →**, **Go To Videos**, or **\+ Start Your Next Video**.

We email you and add an in-app notification when the video is ready. It appears on the Videos page, and the You Shipped screen offers **Download**.

## Other ways in

- The Templates hub lives at app.avatarfactory.io/templates. On the **Video Actions** tab, click **Use with Your Avatar** on a card and the wizard opens with that starter script and direction filled in.
- On any finished video, **Generate Similar →** opens the wizard with the same avatar, script and shot list.
- The wizard saves your draft as you work. Find unfinished drafts under the **Drafts** tab on the Videos page, or accept the resume banner the next time you open the wizard.

## Frequently asked questions

Do I have to write the script myself? +

No. On the Source step you can let the AI content expert brainstorm six hooks and expand the one you pick into a full script, transcribe a reel you paste, or pull a reel from an account you track. Writing from scratch is the fourth option, and every path ends in the same editor.

Can I close the browser while the video renders? +

Yes. Once you confirm the render, the work runs on our side. You can close the tab, refresh, or shut the laptop. We email you and add an in-app notification when the video is ready, and it appears on the Videos page, where you can download it.
