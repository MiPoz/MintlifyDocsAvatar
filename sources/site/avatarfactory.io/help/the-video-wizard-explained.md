# The video wizard explained: Cast, Shots, B-roll, Studio, Writing

What each wizard step does, which steps each template uses, how the Shots puzzle works, and how drafts and the step guard behave.

Videos · Last reviewed September 28, 2026 · 5 min read

## One flow, three templates

Every video walks the same spine: Template, then who is on camera, then Source, Script, and a build step before the render. The template decides which build steps appear:

- **UGC Reel**: Template, Avatar, Source, Script, Shots.
- **Two-Hander Podcast**: Template, Cast, Source, Script, Studio.
- **Split-Window Reel**: Template, Avatar, Source, Script, B-roll, Shots.

The stepper at the top always shows the steps of the template you picked. UGC Reel is available on Starter; the podcast and split-window templates show a **Creator+** pill and open an upgrade prompt on a lower plan.

If you want to see [the finished product](https://avatarfactory.io/ai-avatar-generator) before you read the steps, that page shows what each template produces.

## Template

The first screen, “What are we making today?”, has a **Recreate a reel** field with a **Recreate →** button and one card per template with its name, tagline, and “from ~N credits”. Clicking a card picks it and moves you on.

## Avatar and Cast

UGC and Split-Window use the Avatar step: one avatar on camera. The podcast template uses **Cast** instead: tap one avatar for the host (seated right), tap another for the guest (seated left). Tapping the host again clears the pick. If you own only one avatar, the “AvatarFactory co-hosts” rail seats a house co-host as the guest. **Continue to Source →** unlocks once both seats are filled.

## Source and Writing

Source is where the script comes from: the AI content expert, a pasted reel, a followed account, or a blank page. When you pick an AI hook, the wizard adds a short **Writing** step, “Writing your script.”, where the writer expands the hook. It moves to the editor by itself when the script lands; **← Pick a different idea** takes you back.

## Script

The editor, the word count, the seconds estimate, the Hear It preview, **↺ Revert**, and the Script Co-writer on the right. In the podcast template, speaker tags name who says each line. Template flows can list script rules under the editor; blocking rules disable the continue button until they are fixed, and the co-writer offers to fix them for you.

## Shots (UGC Reel)

The Shots step, “Build your shot puzzle.”, is where the video takes shape. The AI producer reads the script and directs a first cut; the banner above the list shows its progress, then offers **Re-direct All** for another pass and an undo when you want your last cut back.

Each shot card holds:

- **The model.** The cinematic model (“Cinematic motion, camera moves”) or the talking-head model (“Talking head, lip-synced”). The cinematic model shows a **🔒 Creator** lock on Starter. [Render times, models and the receipt](https://avatarfactory.io/help/render-times-models-and-the-receipt) explains both.
- **The scene.** A saved scene from your avatar, a new one generated in the scene drawer, or “Starts where the previous shot ends”, which chains the shot to the last frame of the one before it. When a shot has no scene yet, the **Producer Suggests** panel describes one. On the first shot it offers **Generate This Scene**. On every shot after it, three choices: **Generate This Scene** for a new one, **Continue From Previous Scene** to keep the same set, outfit and framing as the shot before, and **Pick an Existing Scene** for your saved scenes. Continue stays greyed out with “Generate the previous scene first” until the shot before it has a scene. When that last frame shows no face (the shot ended on your hands, or from behind), the next shot starts from the scene the chain began on instead, so the render always has your avatar’s face to work from.
- **The direction and the transcript.** What the avatar does and the words for this slice. Audio tags in brackets shape the delivery and are never spoken or captioned.
- **The length.** A talking-head shot runs 3 to 120 seconds; a cinematic shot runs 3 to 10 seconds.

Above the list, the length meter reads “Video length your plan renders” and turns into “Over the length your plan renders” when the cut is too long. **Shorten to Fit** trims it and offers **Undo** for a few seconds. **Subtitles** is a single **On** / **Off** switch for the whole video; turning it on opens “Choose a caption style” with Studio Pop, Chip, Stamp Drop, Marker Swipe, Mono Bracket and Typewriter.

If some shots still need scenes, a panel offers **Generate N scenes** or **Generate scenes + video**, which generates the scenes and renders in one go. The footer button reads **Generate · N cr** and asks “Start the render?” before charging.

## B-roll and the talking window (Split-Window Reel)

The split-window template cuts the script into two to five chapters. The **B-roll** step shows one clip per chapter that we matched to your words, plus a music bed. On each card, **Change** searches the licensed library and **Generate** edits the AI clip description; **↺ Our pick** restores the suggestion. Matching takes about a minute.

The Shots step for this template, “Frame the talking window.”, has one decision: the image behind your avatar. Choose **Natural backdrop**, one of your saved scenes, or **Generate a scene**. Then click **Animate →** to render.

## Studio (Two-Hander Podcast)

The **Pick your studio.** screen lists your studios, presets and past episodes. **Generate New Studio** opens the studio agent: describe the room in your own words, and it writes the brief, asks you a question when it needs one, and returns empty studio options (the cast is seated later). Click an option to change it in plain words, then **Use this studio**. Below, add an optional prop behind each person and click **✦ Seat the cast · ~6 scene images** to see both people in the room. Each seat has **↻ Re-roll**. **Animate →** renders the episode once the cast is seated.

## Render and Shipped

The oven screen confirms the render is running on our side. When it finishes, the You Shipped screen shows the player, the render receipt and **Download**. [Render times, models and the receipt](https://avatarfactory.io/help/render-times-models-and-the-receipt) reads the receipt line by line.

## Drafts and the step guard

The wizard saves your draft on every step change and after each pause in typing. Unfinished drafts live under **Drafts** on the Videos page, and a plain visit to the wizard offers to resume the newest one.

i

A step cannot open before the steps it depends on. Refreshing on Shots with no script, for example, returns you to Source. This is the step guard, not a lost draft.

## Frequently asked questions

Why did the wizard send me back to the avatar step after a refresh? +

Each step needs the steps before it. If you refresh or open a link to a later step and the draft has no avatar or no script yet, the wizard returns you to the earliest step that is missing something. Your saved draft is not lost; pick it up from the Drafts tab on the Videos page.
