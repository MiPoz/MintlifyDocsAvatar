# Full Clone and Transcribe in the video wizard

Turn any Instagram, TikTok or YouTube link into your avatar's video: Transcribe keeps the words, Full Clone rebuilds the shots, both inside the video wizard.

Videos · Last reviewed September 28, 2026 · 8 min read

## Two ways to copy a reel

Every path below ends in the video wizard with the reel as your source. You choose how deep the copy goes:

- **Transcribe** pulls the words. You get the transcript in the editor, rewrite it in your avatar’s voice, and the wizard’s producer directs the shots from the script. No credits for the transcription.
- **✦ Full Clone · 10 cr** runs a full video analysis: cuts, camera moves, movements and props, shot by shot. The Shots step then replicates the reel’s storyboard with your avatar in the same scene and camera. The 10 credits are charged when the clone starts; the render is priced separately.

Supported links: Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube. The wizard shows which platform it detected under the field. A wrong link gets “Paste a full link starting with https://” or “That link is not a supported Instagram, TikTok or YouTube video.”

For the strategy behind which reel to copy, read [how to recreate viral reels with AI](https://avatarfactory.io/guides/recreate-viral-reels-with-ai).

## Recreate from the template gallery

1. Open **Videos**, click **\+ New Video**.
2. In the **Recreate a reel** field at the top of the gallery, paste the link and click **Recreate →** (or press Enter).
3. Pick your avatar. The wizard opens the Source URL screen and transcribes the reel on its own, showing “Fetching the reel…”, “Transcribing speech…” and the rest.
4. When “Transcript ready” appears with the word count, click **Use this transcript →**.

## Clone from the Source step

1. In the wizard, on the Source step, click **Paste a Reel / TikTok URL**.
2. Paste the link. Click **Transcribe**, or **✦ Full Clone · 10 cr** for the shot-by-shot copy. A clone shows “Downloading the reel…”, “Reading the cuts and camera moves…”, “Building your shot list…” and usually runs 30 to 90 seconds.
3. A finished clone jumps to the Script step by itself with “Cloned from reel” as the source; **Review the cloned script →** takes you there if you went back.
4. On the Shots step, the producer replicates the reel’s shots. Each replicated shot shows the source window it came from. **Re-direct All** re-plans from the reel’s analysis. If the reel’s shots are still being read when you get there, the step shows “Reading the reel’s shots” and builds the storyboard by itself as soon as they are ready, usually within a minute.

Two cases to know:

- **The reel has no speech.** If the words are a background track’s lyrics, the clone says so and leaves the script empty. Write your own script; the Shots step still rebuilds the visuals.
- **The analysis fell back.** If the full analysis could not complete, the screen says “Full video analysis fell back” with the reason. You are charged the completed tier only, and the shots are directed from the script instead.

A clone that fails outright says “We could not process that reel. You were not charged the clone price. Try another link.”

## Who plays whom

On the **Who plays whom?** step, each person found in the reel gets one of your avatars. Tap a face under a person to change who plays them; picking an avatar that already plays someone else swaps the two. Choose **Keep original person** on anyone who is not important to the video: they stay exactly as they are in the reel, face and voice included, and only the people you give an avatar are replaced. Choose **Replace with an avatar** to give them their avatar back. At least one person needs an avatar.

On the Script step, **Hear It** plays only the lines your avatars say. A person you keep speaks in their own voice from the reel, so their lines are left out of the preview, and their words cannot be edited. The same goes for a narrator heard in the original narrator voice.

## Sound

Every **Copy a reel** video is generated with sound. On the Script step, the line under the reel says what the original sounds like:

- **Words and timing from the reel:** someone talks in the reel. Your avatar speaks the script. Music from the original is not copied.
- **Music only, nobody talks:** a song’s lyrics are never turned into a script, and the song itself is not copied. Nobody speaks in your video.
- **Real sounds, nobody talks** (steps, water, tools, a crowd): your video gets the same kind of sounds as the original, and no one speaks.

You can delete every line of the script. Your video is then made with nobody speaking, and the script stays on the page so you can write new lines at any time.

A line marked **Voiceover** is read off camera: your avatar’s voice reads it while the lips stay still. The Script step marks these lines for you. To mark another one when the script shows speaker names, put the cursor right after the line’s @Name and click the **Voiceover** chip. If the Script step asks **Who says this?** about a line, you can also answer **Voiceover, nobody on camera**.

To add music, open your finished video and use **✂ Edit Video**.

## Voices: who speaks as each voice

The **Voices** card on the Script step has one row per voice in the reel (**Voice 1**, **Voice 2** and so on), each with a 3 second sample to play, its number of lines, and the person who speaks as it. Every line follows its voice: click the person on a row and pick someone else, or **Off-screen narrator**, and all of that voice’s lines move at once. To give a single line to someone else, change that line’s @Name in the script; that change still wins for that one line when you move its voice. A voice we could not match to anyone on screen starts as the **Off-screen narrator**. A reel with one voice shows a single line, **1 voice**, with the speaker’s name and **Change**.

## The off-screen narrator

Lines of the **Off-screen narrator** are heard off screen: nobody on camera says them, and every mouth stays still while they play. They show as **@Narrator** in the script. While the narrator speaks, the last row of the **Voices** card, **Narrator’s voice**, picks the voice it is heard in:

- **Keep the original narrator voice** (the default): the narrator’s own lines from the reel, at the same moments. If music plays under them, the voice is lifted off the music. The words cannot be edited in this voice.
- One of your avatars with a voice: the same lines in that avatar’s voice, with the reel’s timing. With an avatar voice you can also rewrite the narrator’s lines.

## Copy from Reel Analytics or an account page

On **Viral Reels**, on an account’s page, and on the Source step’s **From a followed account** screen, every reel carries **⧉ Copy Reel** (the Full Clone, 10 credits, started immediately) and a transcript action. Click either, pick your avatar, and the Source URL screen runs the matching flow with the link already filled in. The clone starts in the background as soon as you click, so part of the work is often done by the time you arrive.

Your copies collect under the **Copied** tab on Reel Analytics with a status of Queued, Copying, Done or Failed. A finished copy offers **New Video From This** and **View Script**; a failed one offers **Retry** and **Cancel**. [Reel analytics and copied reels](https://avatarfactory.io/help/reel-analytics-and-copied-reels) covers that tab.

## When your avatar has no Reference Pack yet

A **Copy a reel** video draws each person from their avatar’s Reference Pack, the sheet of angles on the avatar’s **Reference Pack** tab. If a cast avatar has no pack yet, you do not need to make one first: the copy generates it and waits, and the video card and page read “Preparing the Avatar’s Reference Pack” meanwhile. When the pack is ready the copy carries on by itself. The pack costs the same credits as the one made when an avatar is saved, and you are only charged for it once. If the pack cannot be made, [Rendering failed](https://avatarfactory.io/help/rendering-failed) explains what to do.

## Copy the same reel again

A video made from a clone shows a “Source Reel” card on its page with the platform, the reel length and “Analyzed into N shots”. **Watch Original ↗** opens the reel; **Copy Again** reopens the wizard from the cached analysis with no second clone charge.

## The Chrome extension

There is no AvatarFactory extension on the Chrome Web Store today, so there is nothing to install. If you heard about a **Copy to AvatarFactory** button on Instagram reels, it is not something you can add to your browser yet. This article is where we will write it down when that changes.

You do not need it to copy a reel. Open the reel on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, copy its link from the share menu or the address bar, and paste it into any of the flows above. That is the same copy the button would have started.

If a link is refused, the wizard says “Paste a full link starting with https://” or “That link is not a supported Instagram, TikTok or YouTube video.” Open the reel in a browser first, then copy the full address and paste it again.

## Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Transcribe and Full Clone? +

Transcribe pulls the spoken words only, saves the reel to Reel Analytics, and lets you adapt the script in the editor; it is free. Full Clone costs 10 credits and also breaks the reel into its shots, cuts, camera moves and props, so the Shots step rebuilds the same storyboard with your avatar performing it.

Why doesn't my copy of a music reel have the song? +

The song from the original is never copied, and its lyrics are not something your avatar should say, so nobody speaks in your copy. It is still generated with sound. To add music, open the finished video and use Edit Video.
