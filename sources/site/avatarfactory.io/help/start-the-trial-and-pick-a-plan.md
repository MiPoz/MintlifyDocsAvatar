# Create your account and choose a plan

How to create your AvatarFactory account, choose Starter, Creator or Influencer, add your card, and reach your first avatar in the app.

Getting started · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 4 min read

## Before you start

You need an email address you can access and a card. It is four short steps, and it ends on a checklist that walks you into your first avatar.

## Step 1: Create your account

1. Go to [app.avatarfactory.io/register](https://app.avatarfactory.io/register).
2. Pick how to sign up. **Continue with Google** and **Continue with Meta** sign you in with one click. Or type your email and click **Continue with email**.
3. If you used email, the next page asks for your name and a password. Enter the name you want shown on your dashboard (for example, Sarah Johnson), set a password of at least 6 characters, and click **Create account →**.

i

If you see “Looks like you already have an account. Sign in below.”, you have signed up before. Use the sign-in form on the same page, or **Forgot Password?** to reset it. See [Sign in, email and password](https://avatarfactory.io/help/sign-in-email-and-password).

## Step 2: Pick a plan

The **Pick a plan** page shows three cards: Starter, Creator and Influencer. Every plan starts with the same 1 dollar three-day trial, so choose the plan you want to be on once you are making videos.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly (per month) | Avatars | Monthly credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $39 | $31 | 1 | 325 |
| Creator | $99 | $79 | 3 | 790 |
| Influencer | from $249 | from $199 | Unlimited | 1,490 to 5,990 |

Those are the numbers as of the last review of this article. [The current plan prices](https://avatarfactory.io/pricing) are always on the pricing page and at checkout.

1. Use the **Monthly** / **Annual** toggle at the top. Annual saves 20 percent.
2. On the Influencer card, open the credit tier dropdown to pick 1,490, 2,490, 3,990 or 5,990 credits a month.
3. Click **Start trial for $1** on the card you want.

Not sure? Creator is the plan most creators end up on: three avatars, HD images, auto-publishing and trending intel. Starter is for testing with one avatar and one channel. The full comparison is in [What each plan includes](https://avatarfactory.io/help/what-each-plan-includes).

## Step 3: Add your card

The **Set up payment** page has two parts: **Payment Method** and **Order Summary**.

1. Enter your card in the secure form. AvatarFactory never sees or stores your card details.
2. Check the plan and the billing interval in the summary. **Due Today** shows $1.
3. Have a promo code? Click **Promo code?**, paste it, and click **Apply**. A partner code works the same way through **Have a partner code?** on the plan page.
4. Click **Start 3-Day Trial for $1**.

The 15 trial credits are there to build your avatar. Rendering a video runs on the plan rather than on those credits, so start your plan when you are ready to make one.

> **Warning:** If you see “Your card could not be charged. Please try again.”, the card was declined by your bank. Try another card, or contact your bank and try again. If you see “Card confirmed but we could not start your trial. Contact support.”, write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with your account email and we will finish it on our side.

## Step 4: Four quick questions

Four screens, one question each, and every one has a **Skip** option:

1. **Do you already have an AI influencer?** Pick “I already have an AI influencer / avatar” or “It’s my first AI influencer / avatar”. If you already have one, you can paste a link to it.
2. **What niche are you building in?** Pick a tile. This becomes your default category across the app (templates, followed accounts, Viral Radar). The 18+ niche asks you to confirm your age first.
3. **How did you hear about us?** Pick a source and click **Continue →**.
4. **What are you looking for?** Pick one of five goals: promote my physical or e-commerce product, promote my app, promote digital products, affiliate marketing, or other. After you pick, you can add a short note and a link to what you are promoting. Both are optional. Click **Finish Setup →**.

## What happens next

You land on **Getting Started**, a checklist with three steps: **Create Avatar**, **Generate** your first video, and **Add Accounts** in your niche. Start with the avatar. [Your first avatar](https://avatarfactory.io/help/your-first-avatar) walks you through it.

If you told us you already have an avatar, the checklist points you at **Import your avatar** instead, so you can bring your own face. See [Import your own face](https://avatarfactory.io/help/import-your-own-face).

## Frequently asked questions

What does the trial include? +

The 1 dollar three-day trial comes with 15 credits on the plan you picked. That is enough to build one avatar: three face candidates, a voice, and the reference pack that is created when you save. Rendering a video runs on the plan rather than on the trial credits.

Can I change my plan later? +

Yes. Every plan starts with the same trial, so pick the one that fits your stage and switch later from your Billing page. Upgrades happen right away, downgrades are scheduled, and the Influencer plan has credit tiers you can move between without changing plan. Details are in the billing articles.
