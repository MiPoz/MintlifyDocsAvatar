# Posts, auto-publishing and campaigns: what is live today

Which publishing features work now, which pages the app labels coming soon or Beta, and how to get a finished video onto Instagram or TikTok today.

Growth tools · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 3 min read

## The short version

Making the video is live end to end. Publishing it is manual today: you download it and post it yourself. The pages for scheduled posts, auto-publishing and campaigns exist in the app but are labeled coming soon or Beta, and they do not act on your account yet.

## What works today

Everything you need to get a finished reel onto a platform is on the video page:

1. **⬇ With Captions…** or **⬇ Clean Master (no captions)** downloads the video file (a 1080×1920 MP4).
2. **Social media description** writes and stores a caption: click **Edit**, then **Generate** or type your own, then **Save**. A saved caption has a copy button.
3. **⋯ More** offers **Download Thumbnail** and **Copy Link**.
4. Open Instagram or TikTok, upload the file, paste the caption, and post.

[Download, captions, QA and regenerate](https://avatarfactory.io/help/download-captions-qa-and-regenerate) walks through each of those buttons.

## What the app labels “coming soon”

- **The Publishing card** on every video lists **Instagram** and **TikTok**. Each row reads “connection coming soon” and links to your avatar’s page; the hover text says “Connecting Instagram is coming soon.” (and the same for TikTok).
- **The publish buttons** on the You Shipped screen after a render, **Instagram** and **TikTok**, are disabled and carry a **Soon** pill. **Download** on the same row works.
- **The Posts page** (“Publishing”) opens on “No posts scheduled.” with the line “Instagram and TikTok connections are coming soon. Once they land, generate a video and we’ll publish on a schedule, or kick off a manual post anytime.” Its second button reads **Connect socials · Coming soon** and is not clickable. **Make a video first →** opens the wizard.

## What the app labels “Beta” or sample

Two more pages exist at their addresses but are not in the sidebar today:

- **Auto-Publishing** (the Automation page) shows a “systems running” summary, per-avatar schedules with **Auto-publish** toggles, and an approval queue. Every row is named “Sample Avatar 1” through “Sample Avatar 5”, and the toggles, **Configure new schedule**, **Approve →** and **Approve all** do not run anything on your account. It is a preview of the feature, not a control panel.
- **Campaigns** (Campaign Studio) carries a **★ Beta** badge and describes batch-generating one script across several avatars and scenes. The three campaigns on the page are samples, and **\+ New campaign** does not start one.

i

The sidebar hides the Posts, Automation and Campaign Studio entries while these features are in this state, so you will normally only reach the pages by their address. Nothing on them changes your videos, your credits or your avatars.

## What we do not promise

No dates. When platform connections, scheduling or campaigns go live, the sidebar entries return, the Publishing card changes, and this article is updated in the same release. Until then, treat any “Soon” or “Beta” label as exactly that.

## What to do meanwhile

- Build a posting rhythm from the wizard: **\+ Start Your Next Video** on the oven screen keeps you moving while the previous render runs in the background.
- Use the [QA report](https://avatarfactory.io/help/download-captions-qa-and-regenerate) as your pre-post check: Required Fixes first, then Recommendations.
- Use [Reel Analytics](https://avatarfactory.io/help/reel-analytics-and-copied-reels) to pick what to make next, and the caption generator so every post leaves with a description.
- Read up on [faceless Instagram growth](https://avatarfactory.io/faceless-instagram-ai-growth) for the posting cadence that works while you post by hand.

If you need scheduling for a specific launch and want to know where it stands, write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO). We answer within one business day and will tell you what is live without guessing at dates.
