# Credits and failed renders: they come back automatically

A failed render is never charged. How credit holds work, what reserved, settled, released and expired mean, and when to write to support.

Credits and plans · Last reviewed September 15, 2026 · 3 min read

## The short version

A failed render is never charged. Credits are held when a job starts and only charged when it completes. If the job fails, the hold goes back to your balance on its own. You do not need to write to us for that to happen. The pool a hold comes out of is [your plan’s monthly credits](https://avatarfactory.io/pricing).

## How a charge actually works

Every generation goes through the same three steps:

1. **Reserve.** When you click Generate or Render, the credits the job needs are set aside from your balance. On the **Credit Usage** list on the Plan and Usage page this shows as **reserved**.
2. **Settle.** When the job completes, the hold becomes the final charge and shows as **settled**. For images and voices the settled amount equals the hold. For video it is reconciled to the real rendered length, so it can be a little lower or a little higher than the estimate.
3. **Release.** If the job fails or is canceled, the hold is returned to your balance in full and shows as **released**.

There is a fourth status, **expired**. It marks a leftover hold from a generation that never finished, for example when a job was interrupted before it could report back. Holds like that are returned to your balance automatically. The check runs whenever your balance is read, and a hold is treated as stale after two hours.

## Reading the Credit Usage list

Open **Plan & Usage** and scroll to **Credit Usage**. Each row shows the date, the kind of generation (Image, Scene image, Refine, Video, Avatar finalize), the credit change, the status, and the balance left after it.

- A **settled** row shows a minus, for example “-40 cr”.
- A **released** or **expired** row shows a plus, for example “+40 cr”. That is the hold coming back.

Hover over a status for a one-line explanation of what it means.

## What a failed render looks like

On the Videos page a failed render shows a plain message rather than a technical one. The most common ones:

- “Rendering failed. Try again, and contact support if it keeps happening.”
- “Rendering failed: the video service did not respond. Try again in a minute.”
- “Rendering failed: this avatar has no voice yet. Add a voice, then try again.”
- “Rendering failed: one of the images was rejected. Swap it out, then try again.”
- “Rendering stopped: the script or a shot tripped the content filter. Edit it, then try again.”

In every one of these cases the hold is released. [Rendering failed](https://avatarfactory.io/help/rendering-failed) covers what to change before you try again.

## When to write to us

Write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) if any of these is true:

- A row sits as **reserved** for more than a few hours after the job clearly stopped.
- A **settled** row exists for a video you never received or that is unusable (blank, silent, cut off).
- The balance on the **Credits** card does not match the rows in **Credit Usage**.

Include the email on your account, the video or job ID (it is under the title on the Videos page), and roughly when you started it. We check the job on our side and put the credits back if a settled charge should not have happened. That is the one remedy we can apply without a wait: the Terms say used credits are not refundable, and a failed job never used them.

i

Putting credits back is different from a money refund. Credits are the unit the app spends; a refund is money to your card and follows [the refund policy](https://avatarfactory.io/help/refund-policy-in-plain-words).

## What this does not cover

A render that completed and produced a video you simply do not like is a settled charge. Use the feedback tools on the video to regenerate a shot; each regenerate is its own charge. If you think the result is broken rather than disappointing, send the video ID and we will look at it.

## Frequently asked questions

Do I get my credits back when a render fails? +

Yes, automatically. Credits are held when a generation starts and charged only when it finishes. If the render fails or is canceled, the hold is released back to your balance in full, and the Credit Usage list on the Plan and Usage page shows the line as released. You do not need to ask.

Why does my video charge differ from the amount first held? +

Video credits are reconciled to the real rendered length. The app holds an estimate when you click Render, then settles at the actual seconds produced, so the final charge can be slightly lower or higher than the hold. The Credit Usage list shows the settled amount and the balance left after it.

**Read the ledger yourself** The Credit Usage list on Plan and Usage shows every hold and every release.
