## What Is an AI Avatar Generator?

Quick answer

An AI avatar generator turns a written script into a finished video performed by a consistent synthetic character. You design the avatar once, generate or paste your script, and it renders a vertical, captioned reel in its own face and voice. No camera, no actor, and no editing are needed at any step.

The term covers two very different products, and most of the confusion around it comes from that split. Image avatar makers turn a selfie into stylized portraits: fine for profile pictures, useless for a channel, because the face cannot talk and every new run produces a new stranger. Video avatar generators, this page, produce talking, captioned video from text. A useful test: ask what happens on video twenty. If the tool cannot give you the same character on render twenty as on render one, it is an image toy, not a channel engine.
