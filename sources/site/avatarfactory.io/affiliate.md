## Questions, answered

How much do I earn? +

50% of every payment your referrals make, for as long as they stay subscribed. On a $99 Creator plan that is $49.50 a month per referral.

How long does a referral keep paying me? +

For life. As long as the customer keeps paying AvatarFactory, you keep earning on every renewal and upgrade. There is no 12-month cutoff.

What does my audience get? +

20% off their first 3 months, applied automatically when they sign up through your link. After the $1 trial, their first three payments are 20% cheaper.

How and when am I paid? +

In cash, every month, to your bank account or PayPal. Never as credits or platform balance.

What counts as a referral? +

Anyone who signs up through your link within the tracking window and becomes a paying customer. Renewals and upgrades count too.

How long does the average customer stay? +

8 months on average, and that is the number the calculator uses. Your commission is not capped at 8 months. Some customers stay for years, and you earn on every one of their payments.

Where do I read the full terms? +

At avatarfactory.io/affiliate/terms. It covers the commission, the monthly payout and its $50 minimum, the 30 day hold, how referrals are tracked, and the promotion rules.

Can I run paid ads to my link? +

Yes. Paid ads are allowed. The one rule: do not bid on the AvatarFactory brand name or its misspellings in search ads. Everything else, from YouTube to newsletters to paid social, is welcome.
