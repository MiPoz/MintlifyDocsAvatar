# What’s new

Everything we shipped in AvatarFactory, one entry a week, in plain words.

## Week of September 21, 2026

Shipped September 21 to 25 Latest

- New

 Recreate any reel with your avatars, fully automated

 Paste an Instagram or TikTok link into Recreate a reel, pick Fully automated copy, and seat one of your avatars for every person in it. AvatarFactory rebuilds the whole reel with your avatars, scenes, props, motion and voice included, so there is nothing to edit shot by shot.

- New

 Every avatar gets a wardrobe

 A new Outfits tab on each avatar keeps its outfits, and you can describe new clothes to generate one. When you recreate a reel, you pick an outfit for each avatar or keep the one worn in the original.

- Improved

 Spot the reels that beat their own account

 Reel Analytics has an Outperformers Only switch for reels with at least five times their account's median views at the same age and at least 10,000 views. Each one carries a 5x, 10x or 25x badge, and you can sort the list by Beats Its Account.

 [Outperformers on Reel Analytics](https://avatarfactory.io/help/reel-analytics-and-copied-reels)

- Improved

 Captions at the top, middle or bottom

 The caption picker now sets where the words sit as well as their style, and shows the choice on a frame of your video before anything is burned. Two new styles join the list: Plain, and Solid Box, which reads on any footage.

 [Download with captions](https://avatarfactory.io/help/download-captions-qa-and-regenerate)

- Improved

 Quiet accounts are marked

 A tracked account that stops posting is checked less often, and its row says so, for example "Quiet · checked weekly". The next scan that finds a new reel puts it straight back on the fast schedule.

 [Track competitors](https://avatarfactory.io/help/track-competitors-and-viral-radar)

- Fixed

 The video page works on a phone again

 The shot rail no longer scrolls over Download, Source Reel and Publishing on small screens.

- Fixed

 Copy Reel is easy to see on AI influencer pages

 The Copy Reel button on AI influencer pages was hard to see on the light background. It now uses the standard button style.

- Fixed

 One menu across help, changelog and guides

 The help center, this changelog and every guide share one bar: Help center, Changelog, Guides and Contact.
