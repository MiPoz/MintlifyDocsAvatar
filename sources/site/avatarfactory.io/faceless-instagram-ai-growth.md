## How New Pages Get Out of Instagram Jail

A brand new account starts with almost no distribution. Creators call it Instagram jail: the algorithm has no idea who your page is for yet, so even good reels reach almost nobody at first. Creators who run faceless pages teach three ways out, each with its own trade-off.

First, keyword-stacked captions. Load the caption with the exact terms your niche audience searches, so the algorithm can classify the page fast and push reels to people already interested. Creators report this is working especially well for AI persona accounts right now, and it costs nothing.

Second, buying an account that already has momentum. It skips the cold start, but the same creators who mention it flag it as high risk and say it is usually done wrong. Know it exists, treat it as a last resort.

Third, flooding a new page with targeted traffic so the algorithm gets follower signals early. It only works if the content deserves the reach. One operator reports a reel posted at 2,000 followers that ran past 13 million views and added more than 40,000 followers because the reel itself held up.

### Squeeze Every Winning Reel

When a reel wins, do not repost the same video file. Creators report Instagram detects duplicates and will not push them. Regenerate the reel from the same prompt instead. Every AI generation is unique, so each variant reads as fresh content to the algorithm. One operator says a single reused idea drove about 65 million of an 85 million view month.

Trial reels are the other lever. Instagram shows a trial reel only to people who do not follow you, so you can re-run a proven winner to fresh audiences without spamming your own followers. Repeat until the idea stops working, then move to the next winner.
