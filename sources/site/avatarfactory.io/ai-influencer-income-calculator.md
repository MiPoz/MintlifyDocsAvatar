## The Five Formulas, In Plain Math

Each path has its own formula and its own platform take rate. Here is exactly what runs under each output. Every figure is an estimate, before churn and taxes.

### Subscriptions

A recurring monthly figure on a platform like Fanvue or Passes (90/10 split, you keep 90 percent).

`Subscribers = Followers x Conversion rate``Monthly revenue you keep = Subscribers x Price x 0.90`

### Affiliate (Per Reel)

A per-reel figure, since affiliate income is event-driven, not recurring.

`Sales = Clicks x Conversion rate``Revenue per reel = Sales x (Product price x Commission rate)`

### Digital Products (Per Reel)

The highest-margin path, since no one takes a percentage of the sale beyond a small flat storefront fee like Stan’s ~$29 a month.

`Sales = Storefront visits x Conversion rate``Revenue per reel = Sales x Price, minus the flat monthly storefront fee`

### Paid Community (Monthly Recurring)

A recurring figure on a platform like Skool ($99 tier, 2.9 percent fee) or Whop (2.7 percent plus $0.30 per sale).

`Members = Followers x Conversion rate``Monthly revenue you keep = Members x Price x (1 minus platform fee)`

### Platform Ad Revenue (Per Million Views)

The one path where you sell nothing. The platform pays a share of the ads it runs against your views.

`Revenue = (Views / 1,000) x RPM x Creator share`

For YouTube long-form the creator share is 0.55 (YouTube keeps 45 percent). TikTok Creator Rewards has no separate split, the RPM is already your take.

## Worked Example, End To End

Take the realistic default: a persona with 50,000 engaged followers. Watch the same audience produce wildly different monthly numbers depending on the path you point it at.

**Subscriptions.** 50,000 x 1% = 500 subscribers. 500 x $14.99 x 0.90 = about $6,745 a month you keep on Fanvue. Run the identical audience on OnlyFans (80/20) and it drops to about $5,996. Same 500 subs, a $749 swing, decided by the platform split alone.

**Paid community.** 50,000 x 1% = 500 members at $47 on Skool's $99 tier. 500 x $47 x 0.971 = about $22,819 a month you keep. Same audience, same conversion, more than three times the subscription figure, because the price you control is higher.

**Affiliate (per reel).** A single reel drives 2,000 clicks at a 3 percent conversion to a $40 product at 15 percent commission. 2,000 x 3% = 60 sales. 60 x ($40 x 15%) = about $360 per reel. Post twenty a month and that is roughly $7,200, before you optimize a single hook.

**Digital products (per reel).** A reel drives 1,000 storefront visits at 4 percent to a $27 ebook. 1,000 x 4% = 40 sales. 40 x $27 = $1,080, minus the ~$29 flat Stan fee spread across the month, so about $1,051 per reel kept. The highest margin on the list, because no one takes a percentage of the sale.

**Ad revenue.** 1,000,000 views on a general YouTube channel at a $5 RPM and the 55 percent creator share. (1,000,000 / 1,000) x $5 x 0.55 = about $2,750. Move that same million views to a faceless finance channel at a $20 RPM and it becomes about $11,000. Same effort, a 4x swing, decided by niche.

One audience. Five very different paychecks. The persona earns the audience. The path you point it at, and the price you set, is what turns it into income.
