## Fashion AI influencers, answered.

Who is the biggest fashion AI influencer? +

Lil Miquela is the biggest fashion AI influencer, with about 2.2 million Instagram and 3.3 million TikTok followers as of June 2026, and a campaign history covering Calvin Klein, Prada, Samsung, and BMW. Noonoouri is the category number two, with an IMG Models contract and a Warner Music record deal.

How do fashion AI influencers make money? +

Fashion AI influencers earn mainly through brand campaigns: luxury houses, beauty lines, and automotive brands book them like human models. Fees are rarely disclosed; third party estimates for the top names run from a few thousand dollars per post to six figure campaign deals. Modeling contracts and music deals layer on top.

Why do fashion brands work with AI influencers? +

Control and press. A synthetic model gives a campaign exact visual control, never misses a shoot, and generates earned media simply for being AI. Prada, Balmain, Calvin Klein, and Dior all ran AI model campaigns that were covered far beyond their paid reach, which is part of what the brand pays for.
