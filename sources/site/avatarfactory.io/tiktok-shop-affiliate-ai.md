## From Commission to Brand Deals

Affiliate commission is the floor, not the ceiling. The bigger income lives one step past it, and you reach it without changing a thing about how you post.

Affiliate commission, whether TikTok Shop or Amazon Associates, is a small percentage and it is reactive. You only earn when a viewer buys through your exact link inside the attribution window. No tracked click in that window, no commission. That percentage is a ceiling the platform sets, and there is no negotiating it up.

So flip what the tracked link is for. Keep running the same comment-to-DM (or shop-link) funnel, but treat the link as a measurement tool, not the paycheck. It is proof that your page sends real buyers. Every settled sale is a data point you own: this face, this niche, this cadence, drives clicks that convert.

At month end, take that proof to the brand directly. A brand that can see your page convert is buying a different deal than the affiliate program offers. Instead of a reactive percentage, it pays a flat retainer per post, plus bonuses for clicks and conversions. That is proactive and uncapped, and it renews higher as the page keeps proving out. Then you stack pages across niches and brands, and each proven page is leverage for the next deal. Creators report performance bonuses well into five figures for a strong click month.
