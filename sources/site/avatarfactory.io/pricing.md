## Pricing 
**questions**

If we didn't cover yours, ping us and we'll add it.

How does the $1 trial work? ![](https://avatarfactory.io/v2/union-14x14-x4254-y6733.svg) 

Full access to the plan you pick for 3 days. After 72 hours, it auto-converts to that plan's monthly or yearly price. Cancel in 1 click from your settings and you are not charged. Anything you built is yours to keep.

Can I switch plans later? ![](https://avatarfactory.io/v2/union-14x14-x4254-y6733.svg) 

Yes, anytime. Upgrade and you keep working. Downgrade and the change applies at the start of the next billing cycle. We do not pro-rate punishments.

How many videos do I actually get? ![](https://avatarfactory.io/v2/union-14x14-x4254-y6733.svg) 

Starter ships 30 videos a month, Creator 150, Influencer 500. Each video can be up to 60 seconds. Image generation runs in the same pipeline and is counted separately (roughly twice the video count, per plan). The compare table above has the exact numbers.

What happens if I hit my monthly limit? ![](https://avatarfactory.io/v2/union-14x14-x4254-y6733.svg) 

You can buy a top-up pack at any time without changing your plan. Or upgrade to the next tier and the remaining allowance carries over for the rest of the billing period.

Yearly vs monthly · what is the discount? ![](https://avatarfactory.io/v2/union-14x14-x4254-y6733.svg) 

Yearly billing saves you 20% over month-to-month, paid upfront. You can still cancel during your trial and not be charged.

Do you offer refunds? ![](https://avatarfactory.io/v2/union-14x14-x4254-y6733.svg) 

The trial exists so you do not need one. If something is genuinely broken, email us and we will sort it.

Is there an enterprise tier? ![](https://avatarfactory.io/v2/union-14x14-x4254-y6733.svg) 

Influencer covers most agencies and studios. If you need SSO, custom contracts, or dedicated support for a team of 20+, reply to your trial email and we will set up a call.

More answers, written from the product: [the help center](https://avatarfactory.io/help). Start with [what your plan includes](https://avatarfactory.io/help/what-each-plan-includes) and [how credits work](https://avatarfactory.io/help/how-credits-work).
