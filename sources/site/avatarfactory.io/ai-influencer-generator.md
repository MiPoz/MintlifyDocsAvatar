## What the Algorithm Actually Rewards in 2026

"Post daily" is half the advice. The other half is knowing what the feed actually scores, and in 2026 it is not likes. The priority, in order, is watch time, then shares, then saves, then likes.

A like is the cheapest signal a viewer can give. A save means "I need this again." A share means "I will spend my own reputation on this." Those are the signals that push a post past your own followers.

Here are the rough benchmarks creators reverse-engineer from live accounts. Treat them as directional, not exact.

| Signal | Average Post | Viral Post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average watch time | 3 to 5 seconds | 22 to 36 seconds |
| Save rate | about 1 in 30 viewers | about 1 in 10 viewers |
| Reshare rate | about 2 percent | 5 to 10 percent |

Hit two of those three and the algorithm does the distribution for you. This is exactly where the 500 million reels we analyzed earn their keep: the script generation already structures content the way winning posts are built, so the open-loop hook, the reason to keep watching, and the save-worthy payoff are in the draft before you change a word.

The practical move is simple. Stop optimizing for likes. Write the hook to survive the first three seconds, give the viewer one thing worth saving, and end on a line worth sending to a friend.

RPM is what you earn per 1,000 monetized views, and the gap between formats is huge. Shorts pay roughly 3 to 10 cents per 1,000 views. Long-form pays $2 to $15-plus per 1,000, because it carries more ad slots. The niche stacks on top: a finance, US, or 40-plus audience commands premium RPM, while broad entertainment sits at the floor. The play is long-form in a premium niche, which is exactly the [faceless YouTube channel](https://avatarfactory.io/faceless-youtube-channel-ai) setup.

| Long-form, mid niche | $2 to $8 | Watch time, ad slots |
| Long-form, finance/US/older | $8 to $15-plus | Premium advertiser demand |

### TikTok Creativity Program

TikTok pays creators directly for qualifying long-form video (over one minute) through its rewards program, on a per-view basis that rewards original, watch-worthy content. It is a separate rail from brand deals, so it stacks on top of everything else the persona earns.
