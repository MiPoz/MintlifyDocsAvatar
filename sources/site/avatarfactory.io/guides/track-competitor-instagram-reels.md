## Why tracking beats scrolling

Opening Instagram and browsing competitor profiles feels like research, but it fails in three ways. You only see what the algorithm shows you. You have no memory of what a reel did last week versus today. And you cannot compare twenty accounts side by side in your head.

A tracker fixes all three. It watches the accounts you choose, logs every reel’s numbers over time, and puts the whole niche in one sortable table. The result is a private leaderboard of what your market rewards right now.

## Step 3: Read the right numbers

Raw view counts flatter old posts. The metrics that matter:

**Views per hour.** The single best early signal. It divides current views by hours since posting, so a fresh breakout beats a stale giant. This is the default sort in AvatarFactory’s Viral Reels feed.

**Comments.** A reel that makes people type is a reel that provokes. Formats that provoke get shared and stitched. Sort by comments to find discussion magnets that pure view counts miss.

**Engagement rate.** Likes plus comments divided by views. It levels the field between a 10,000 follower account and a 2 million follower account, so you can spot a small creator whose format punches far above their size. Those are the formats with room left.

## Step 4: Hunt outliers, not averages

Once the feed fills up, filter it. Set a minimum views threshold so noise disappears, narrow to the last 7 days, and sort by views per hour. What remains is the short list of reels your niche is rewarding this week.

The pattern to look for is the outlier: a reel doing 5x or 10x the account’s normal numbers. Average reels tell you what an audience tolerates. Outliers tell you what it wants more of. When the same format is an outlier on two different accounts, that is not luck, that is a wave you can still catch.

Can I see how many views a competitor's reel gets per hour? +

Yes. Views per hour is the headline metric in AvatarFactory's Viral Reels feed. It divides a reel's current view count by the hours since it was posted, which exposes fresh breakouts that raw view counts hide. A day-old reel at 40,000 views is outperforming a month-old reel at 300,000.

Which reel metrics actually predict a viral format? +

Watch views per hour first, then comments. High comments relative to views means the hook provokes a response, and formats that provoke responses travel across audiences. Likes confirm reach but follow the other two. Engagement rate ties them together by dividing likes plus comments by views, so you can compare small and large accounts fairly.

Is it legal to track a competitor's public Instagram account? +

Yes. Public profiles publish their reels and view counts to everyone, and reading public data is standard competitive research that every marketing team does. What copyright protects is the video file itself. Track the numbers freely, study the formats, and when you recreate a winner, produce your own original video rather than reposting theirs.
