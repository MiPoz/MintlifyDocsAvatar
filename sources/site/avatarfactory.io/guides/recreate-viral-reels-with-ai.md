## Copying a Reel vs Downloading It vs Reposting It

These three sound similar and are completely different in law, in risk, and in what Instagram does with the result.

?

**What is Copy Reel?**

Copy Reel is the AvatarFactory feature that reads a competitor’s viral reel, transcribes its script, and loads that transcript into the video wizard so your own AI avatar can perform an improved version. It never saves or re-uploads the original file. You copy the structure, not the video.

**Downloading** saves the creator’s exact video file to your device. **Reposting** takes that same file and publishes it under your name. Both put another person’s footage on your account, both carry copyright risk, and both trip Instagram’s duplicate-upload detection. **Recreating** with Copy Reel does none of that. AvatarFactory never stores or re-uploads anyone’s clip. It transcribes the words, you rewrite them, and your avatar renders a brand new video. The only thing that carries over is the idea, and ideas are not what copyright protects.

## Is It Legal to Recreate Someone Else’s Reel?

Copyright protects the specific expression of a work, the exact footage, the exact edit, the exact word-for-word script. It does not protect the idea, the format, or the concept behind it. That distinction is the whole game. Copying the footage or the script verbatim is infringement. Recreating the concept with your own script, your own voice, and your own avatar is the legal path, and it is the one Copy Reel is built for: your persona, your rewritten words, an original render.

Two habits keep you clean. First, always rewrite the script rather than reciting it word for word. Second, disclose that the content is AI-generated where the platform requires it. ## The Mistakes That Turn Inspired By Into Ripped Off

Recreation is legal and effective right up until you cut a corner. These four turn a fresh original into a copy:

- **Copying the script word for word.** Reciting the exact transcript is the closest thing to lifting the footage. Rewrite the hook and the payoff every time.
- **Reusing the original footage.** The moment any of the creator’s actual clip appears in your video, you are reposting, not recreating. Copy Reel never touches the file, so do not add it back.
- **Skipping the AI label.** Disclosure is cheap and non-optional. Leaving it off risks the account for no upside.
- **Rotating the avatar face.** Every new face resets the recognition your page exists to build. One host, every reel, without exception.

Get those right and the model is simple: find the reel that is already winning, keep its structure, ship it in your own persona’s voice, and let recognition compound. Recreate the idea. Never the file.
