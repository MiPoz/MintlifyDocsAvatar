## How to Make an AI Influencer With Claude, Step by Step

Six steps take you from an empty chat to a published reel fronted by a persona you own.

### Step 1: Connect AvatarFactory to Claude

Open Claude’s Settings, go to Connectors, and click Add custom connector. Paste the AvatarFactory MCP URL and approve it. On Claude Code you do the same thing from the terminal with a single add command. From this point on, Claude can reach every AvatarFactory tool inside the chat.

### Step 2: Ask Claude what is working in your niche

Tell Claude your niche and ask what is trending. Claude calls the trend tool, which draws from more than 500 million analyzed reels, and hands back the hooks, formats, and angles catching fire right now. ### Step 3: Shape the persona

Ask Claude to turn the niche into a full AI influencer concept. It drafts the face, the voice, and the point of view. Push back, refine it, and lock the version you like. The persona is the asset, so spend a minute here. The same character will front every video you make.

### Step 5: Build the avatar

Tell Claude to build the avatar. AvatarFactory generates the consistent face for your persona, the one that returns in every reel. Your first 2 images are free, so you can see the character before you commit to anything.

### Step 6: Render the video

## Why the Persona Has to Stay the Same

Anyone can generate one AI face. What builds an audience is the same face showing up every day. When the persona is consistent, three things happen: the viewer starts to trust the character, the algorithm learns who the account is for, and the persona becomes a brand you can put an offer behind. A new face every prompt resets all three to zero.

That is why AvatarFactory locks the face and the voice when you build the persona, and why Claude reuses that exact persona every time you ask for a new video. You are not generating strangers. ## The Mistakes That Waste the Workflow

Four habits turn a clean build into a dead channel:

- **Rotating the face.** Every new face resets the recognition the persona exists to build. Lock one character and reuse it in every reel.
- **Skipping the trend pull.** If you write the script cold, you throw away the one thing Claude alone cannot know. Ask what is working first, then write.
- **Shipping the first draft unread.** The trend data structures the script, but your voice and your offer make it yours. Read it, tweak the hook, then render.
- **Posting once and stopping.** One reel is a test, not a channel. The model works because the same persona posts on a cadence. Batch a week of scripts in one Claude session.

Get those right and the loop is simple: ask what is working, shape the persona, write the reel, render it, publish, repeat. Same face every time, and let recognition compound.
