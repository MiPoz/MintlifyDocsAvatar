## Platform Comparison: Where to Post First

All three short-form platforms reward the same things, volume and consistency, but they pay differently. The practical answer for most operators is to post everywhere and pick one platform as the primary based on the niche and the payout mechanics.

| | Instagram Reels | TikTok | YouTube Shorts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Native monetization threshold | No reliable native payout program; treat Instagram as affiliate and brand deal territory | Creator Rewards Program: roughly 10K followers and 100K views in the last 30 days | Partner Program: 1K subscribers plus 10M public Shorts views in 90 days, or 4K long-form watch hours |
| Payout speed | Set by your deals and affiliate programs, not the platform | Monthly payouts once accepted into the program | Monthly via AdSense once accepted into the Partner Program |
| Affiliate friendliness | Strong: link in bio, stories, and DM automation are standard practice | Good: TikTok Shop plus bio links, with strict disclosure rules | Strong: description links on every video keep earning with the catalog |
| Best niche fit | Lifestyle, fitness, and visual persona brands | Trend-led, personality-driven, street and reaction formats | Search-driven topics: finance, history, how-to, tech explainers |

Thresholds are the publicly documented requirements as of mid 2026 and change over time; always confirm against the official platform pages linked in the Sources below.
