Everything about your subscription lives in **Plan & Usage** — switching plans, reviewing billing history, downloading invoices, and updating your card. Canceling and pausing both live in **Settings**. Read through the options below before you make a change, because upgrades, downgrades, pauses, and cancellations each work differently.

## Upgrade to a higher plan

Upgrades apply immediately. The price difference is charged now, the extra credits land in your balance right away, and your renewal date does not move.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plan & Usage">
    Click **Plan & Usage** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the plan you want">
    Under **Other plans**, locate the plan. On the Influencer card, select a credit tier from the **Monthly credits** dropdown first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Switch">
    The **Confirm Your Switch** summary opens. Review the four lines: **Plan**, **Charged now** (the price difference), **Credits added now** (the extra credits you receive on confirm), and **Payment method**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the upgrade">
    Click **Confirm**. The charge is processed, the new plan unlocks immediately, and the credits are added to your balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  To pay with a different card, click **Change payment method** in the summary before confirming. The new card becomes the default for future renewals.
</Note>

## Downgrade to a lower plan

Downgrades are scheduled to take effect at the end of your current paid term. Nothing is charged today and your current plan continues until the scheduled date.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plan & Usage">
    Click **Plan & Usage** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Switch on the lower plan">
    The **Schedule Plan Change** summary opens and shows **Due today: $0** and the **Starts on** date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Schedule Downgrade">
    A banner appears at the top of the page confirming the scheduled change. You keep your current plan until the effective date. To cancel the downgrade before it takes effect, click **Cancel Downgrade** on the banner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pause for 30 days

A pause stops billing for 30 days at no cost. Your avatars, videos, settings, and designed voices stay exactly as they are. Rendering and creating are on hold while the pause runs. The plan resumes automatically when the 30 days are up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click **Settings** in the sidebar and scroll to the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete account">
    This opens the account exit flow. The very first screen offers a pause — you do not need to go further.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Pause for 30 Days">
    A confirmation screen shows the date the pause ends. A ribbon at the top of the app confirms the pause is active and shows the resume date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you want to return before the 30 days are up, click **Resume Now** on the ribbon. Billing and access restart immediately.
</Tip>

**Pause vs. cancel at a glance:**

| | Pause | Cancel |
|---|---|---|
| Charges | None for 30 days | None after current paid term |
| Duration | 30 days, then auto-resume | Ends at end of paid term |
| Avatars and videos | Kept | Kept |
| Designed voices | **Kept** | **Deleted — cannot be restored** |
| Coming back | One click, everything as you left it | New subscription, new voice |

## Cancel your subscription

<Warning>
  Canceling permanently deletes the voices you designed — they cannot be restored. If there is any chance you will return, choose the free 30-day pause instead.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click **Settings** in the sidebar and scroll to the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete account">
    The first screen offers a pause. Click **Continue to cancel** to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a reason (optional)">
    Pick a reason from the list. You can add a sentence in the text box — it goes directly to the founders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decline the keep-my-account offers">
    The last screen shows three offers (discount, reduced-price month, and free pause). Click **No thanks, cancel my subscription** to complete the cancellation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After canceling: you keep full access until the end of the billing term you paid for; no further charge is made; your avatars and videos remain saved; designed voices are deleted.

<Note>
  Canceling stops future charges — it is not a refund. If you believe your last charge qualifies for a refund, submit a request within the applicable window (see below).
</Note>

## Refund policy

The refund windows below are a plain-language summary of Terms sections 3.3 and 4. The Terms are the source of record — if anything conflicts, the Terms win.

| Charge | Refund window |
|---|---|
| Monthly plan payment | 24 hours from payment |
| Annual plan payment | 7 days from payment |
| Credit purchase | 24 hours (credits must be unused) |
| Add-on | No refund |
| Any charge older than 7 days | No refund |

Each renewal starts its own window — a monthly renewal is eligible within 24 hours of that renewal payment. Approved refunds may be reduced by up to 15% for payment processing fees and are returned to the original payment method.

**To request a refund:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the charge in Billing History">
    Open **Plan & Usage** → scroll to **Billing History** → note the exact date and amount of the charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email support">
    Write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) from your account email. Include the charge date, amount, and a brief reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    Support replies within one business day. A refund is confirmed in writing before it is issued.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Contact support **before** filing a dispute with your bank. Filing a chargeback without contacting us first — or filing one that is determined to be without merit — may result in account suspension and forfeiture of remaining credits.
</Warning>

## Invoices and billing history

Every payment is listed in **Plan & Usage → Billing History**. Invoices are not sent to your email automatically — download them from this page.

- Click a row description to open the hosted invoice in a new tab.
- Click **PDF ↓** on the right to download it directly.
- Rows show: date, description, amount with currency, and status (paid / succeeded / refund).

Your card details are stored securely by the payment processor — Avatar Factory never stores your full card number. To update the card on file, click **Switch** on any plan in Plan & Usage, then click **Change payment method** in the summary. Save the new card, then click **Cancel** to close the summary without switching plans.
