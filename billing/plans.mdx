## Annual discount

Switching from monthly to annual applies immediately and is treated as an upgrade — you are charged the price difference today and your credits continue to arrive month by month. To switch, open **Plan & Usage**, then click **Switch to Annual · Save 20%** on your current plan card.

## How upgrades and downgrades work

Upgrades apply immediately. The price difference is charged now, the extra credits land in your balance right away, and your renewal date does not move. Downgrades are scheduled to take effect at the end of your current paid term — nothing is charged today and your current plan continues until that date.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change plans at any time?">
    Yes. Open **Plan & Usage** and click **Switch** on any plan. Upgrades apply immediately with no change to your renewal date. Downgrades are scheduled for the end of your current term, with nothing charged today. You can cancel a scheduled downgrade any time before it takes effect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does upgrading raise the daily render limit?">
    No. Every plan has the same 10-render-per-day limit, counted from midnight UTC. Upgrading adds credits to your monthly pool but does not change the daily render cap. If you need a higher daily limit, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an enterprise or custom plan?">
    Influencer covers most agencies and studios. If you need SSO, custom contracts, or dedicated support for a team of 20 or more, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with your requirements and we will set up a call.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
