Avatar Factory support is one inbox, read by a real person. The more context you include in your first message, the fewer follow-up questions come back and the faster your issue is resolved. Support responds within one business day — usually the same day.

## How to reach support

**Email:** [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO)

**Contact form:** [avatarfactory.io/help/contact](https://avatarfactory.io/help/contact)

Both the form and the email reach the same inbox.

<Tip>
  Check the relevant troubleshooting page first — they cover every error message in the app and usually resolve the issue faster than waiting for a reply.
</Tip>

## What to include for fastest resolution

Sending these details in your first message means support can act without a back-and-forth:

1. **Account email** — the address you signed up with, so we can find your account.
2. **What you were trying to do** — one line describing the screen or feature you were using.
3. **Exact error message** — copy-paste the full text; the wording tells us which failure it was.
4. **Video or job ID** — open the video in the app; the ID is the last segment of the page address after `/app/videos/` (for example `/app/videos/abc123def` → ID is `abc123def`).
5. **Approximate time and timezone** — when the issue occurred.
6. **Screenshot (optional)** — PNG or JPG, up to 15 MB.

## Topic guide

Use this table to pick the right category when submitting the contact form, so your message reaches the right person.

| Topic | Examples |
|---|---|
| Render or video failure | Failed video, stuck render, missing notification |
| Avatar, face, or voice problem | Generation error, face drift, voice not working |
| Credits, limits, or plan contents | Balance wrong, hit daily limit, credit not returned |
| Billing, invoices, or card | Charge question, refund request, update card |
| Sign-in or account access | Can't log in, wrong email, session issues |
| API or integration | MCP connector not working, API access request |
| Custom plan or partnership | Team 20+, SSO, bulk credits |

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I request a refund by email?">
    Yes. Email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) from your account email address with the charge date, the amount, and a brief reason. Support checks the charge against the refund windows and replies within one business day. A refund is confirmed in writing before it is issued. See the [Refund Policy](/policies/refund-policy) for the applicable windows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is my video ID and where do I find it?">
    Open the video in the app. The ID is the last segment of the page address after `/app/videos/`. For example, if the URL is `/app/videos/abc123def`, your video ID is `abc123def`. Include this in any support message about a specific video or render.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How quickly will support reply?">
    Within one business day. Most replies arrive the same day. If nothing has landed by the next day, check your spam folder — replies come from the same AvatarFactory.IO domain.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
