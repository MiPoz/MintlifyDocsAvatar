Most face and scene generation failures are one-off. Click **Try again** on the failed tile — or resend the prompt in Image Studio — and it usually works on the next attempt. A generation that fails should not use a credit. If the same error keeps appearing, work through the ordered fixes below from most to least likely.

## Error messages and what they mean

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Couldn't generate this face (one tile)">
    **Where:** Face step in the avatar wizard — one tile in the batch.

    **What it means:** That single candidate failed. The other two tiles in the batch are untouched.

    **Fix:** Click **Try again** under the failed tile. Each retry is a new generation at 1 credit. Fix the description before retrying multiple times.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Face generation failed">
    **Where:** Face step — the whole batch was rejected.

    **What it means:** All three candidates were refused, usually because the description contains something the content filter blocks.

    **Fix:** Simplify the description — remove real celebrity names, brand logos, contradictions, or anything against the content rules — then try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Face generation timed out">
    **Where:** Face step.

    **What it means:** The batch ran past eight minutes without returning a result.

    **Fix:** Leave the tab open during generation — closing the tab and reopening it reattaches to the running job instead of starting a new one. If the timeout happens again, refresh the page and check the grid; the image may already be there. Then try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The refine failed — try again in a moment">
    **Where:** Face step, refine sheet.

    **What it means:** The refine call did not return. Your original face is kept intact — nothing was overwritten.

    **Fix:** Wait a moment and click **Try again**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Uploading your photo failed">
    **Where:** Face step or the import photo step.

    **What it means:** The file did not reach the server.

    **Fix:** Check the file format — PNG, JPG, WebP, and HEIC are accepted — and the file size (maximum 15 MB). A blurry or heavily filtered photo can also cause failure. Fix the file and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Generation failed or timed out (Image Studio)">
    **Where:** Image Studio — scene or background generation.

    **What it means:** The scene render was rejected or ran past eight minutes.

    **Fix:** Click **Try again**. If it fails repeatedly, simplify the prompt — remove contradictions, real names, or flagged content — and send it again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Upgrade to a paid plan to create scenes and videos">
    **Where:** Image Studio or video wizard.

    **What it means:** Your account is on the free allowance (2 images + 5 scripts total) and has not started the paid trial yet.

    **Fix:** Open **Plan & Usage** and start the $1 three-day trial. It includes 15 credits and unlocks scenes and video.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Fix steps in order of likelihood

Work through these in order when generation keeps failing:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Try once more">
    Click **Try again** on the failed tile or resend the prompt. Give the countdown time to finish — a face batch starts near "~75s left" and holds near 10 seconds at the end while the final result lands. That pause is normal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the tab open during face batch generation">
    If you close the tab, reopening the wizard reattaches to the running job instead of starting a new one. Leave the tab open until the batch completes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Simplify the description">
    Remove contradictions (for example, "bald, long hair"), real celebrity names, brand logos, or anything the content rules block. Flagged descriptions fail every time regardless of how many retries you attempt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the reference image">
    In Exact match mode and in the import wizard, the file must be PNG, JPG, WebP, or HEIC, and under 15 MB. A tiny, blurry, or heavily filtered photo can produce a failed face. Fix the file before retrying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your connection">
    A timeout often means the browser lost the server poll. Refresh the page and check the grid — the image may already be there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your credit balance">
    Open **Plan & Usage**. If your balance is zero, generation stops. See [Out of Credits or Hit the Daily Render Limit](/troubleshooting/credits-and-limits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait a few minutes">
    If several generations fail in a row with the same message, the image service may be temporarily down. Wait a few minutes and try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Each **Try again** on a face tile is a new generation. A successful retry costs 1 credit. Retrying five times on a description that will never pass the content filter costs five credits. Fix the description first.
</Warning>

## Credits and failed generations

A generation that failed and produced nothing should not use a credit. If your balance dropped after a failed or timed-out generation, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with the details below. Credits for failed generations are returned — that is the policy, no argument needed.

## Contacting support

If the issue persists after working through the steps above, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with:

- Your account email address
- The avatar name and what you were generating (face, scene, or background)
- The exact error message, or a screenshot
- The approximate time and your timezone
- Your device and browser

Support replies within one business day.
