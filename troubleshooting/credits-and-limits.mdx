Avatar Factory checks the full cost of an action before it starts, so you will never get a half-finished batch of images or a partial video due to an empty balance. When the app stops you, the message tells you exactly which limit you hit and what to do next. Each message has a different cause and a different fix.

## Out-of-credits, limit, and plan messages

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='Out of credits: Upgrade your plan to get more credits'>
    **What it means:** Your monthly credit pool is empty. The app checked the full cost of the action against your balance before starting and stopped to protect you from a half-completed job.

    **Fastest fix — upgrade now:**
    1. Click **Upgrade to** on the prompt, or open **Plan & Usage** and click **Switch** on the next plan.
    2. Review the summary: **Charged now** is the price difference, **Credits added now** is the extra credits you receive the moment you confirm.
    3. Click **Confirm**. New credits land in your balance immediately and your renewal date does not move.

    **If you are already on the top Influencer tier:** there is no plan above you. The prompt shows your next refill date instead of an upgrade button. Open **Plan & Usage → Credits** to see the **Next refill** date.

    <Tip>
      Before upgrading, check the **Credit Usage** list in Plan & Usage. Failed generations show as **released** and those credits are already back in your balance. A hold that is still showing as **reserved** long after a job stopped will return automatically within two hours.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title='Daily render limit reached: resets tomorrow at midnight UTC'>
    **What it means:** You have used all 10 video renders for the current UTC day. The daily render limit is separate from your credit balance — you can have plenty of credits and still see this message.

    **Fastest fix — use the time productively:**
    - Wait for the daily count to reset. The day runs on UTC, so "tomorrow" starts at midnight UTC, not midnight in your local timezone.
    - Use the waiting time on work that does not count as a render: write and polish scripts with the AI co-writer (50 turns/day, free), generate scene images, or design and preview voices.

    <Note>
      Upgrading your plan does **not** raise the daily render limit. Every plan has the same 10-render-per-day cap. Upgrading adds credits to your monthly pool, not renders to your daily cap. To request a higher daily limit, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with your account email and the number of renders per day you need.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Free allowance used: start the $1 trial to continue">
    **What it means:** Your account has not started the paid trial yet. Free accounts can generate 2 images and write 5 scripts in total before this message appears on every generation attempt.

    **Fix:**
    1. Click the button on the message, or open **Plan & Usage**.
    2. Start the $1 three-day trial. It comes with 15 credits and unlocks scene generation, video, and all plan features.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Over the length your plan renders (video wizard)">
    **What it means:** You are on Starter, which caps video renders at 45 seconds. The shot meter in the wizard shows the current cut length against the cap, for example "52s of 45s".

    **Fix — option 1:** Click **Shorten to Fit**. The wizard trims the longest shots down to fit within 45 seconds. A toast confirms the trim and offers **Undo** if you want to review the change.

    **Fix — option 2:** Upgrade to Creator or Influencer, which remove the 45-second cap entirely.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Daily render limit — important note

<Note>
  The 10-render-per-day limit applies on every plan and is counted from midnight UTC. Upgrading your plan adds credits to your monthly pool but does not increase the daily render cap. If your workflow regularly needs more than 10 renders per day, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) to request a higher limit.
</Note>

## Still stuck?

If your credit balance does not match what you have made, or the daily limit message appears before you have rendered ten videos that day, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with:

- Your account email address
- The exact message you saw
- The time it happened and your timezone

Support checks the ledger on our side and replies within one business day.
