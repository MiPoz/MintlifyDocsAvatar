Setting up your Avatar Factory account takes about five minutes. You create an account, pick a plan, add a card (just $1 today), answer a few optional questions about your goals, and land on a checklist that walks you into your first avatar. This page covers every screen in that flow.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.avatarfactory.io/register](https://app.avatarfactory.io/register). You have three ways to sign up:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Continue with Google">
        Click **Continue with Google** and select your Google account. Sign-up completes in one click — no password to set.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Continue with Meta">
        Click **Continue with Meta** and authorize with your Facebook account. Sign-up completes in one click — no password to set.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Continue with email">
        1. Type your email address and click **Continue with email**.
        2. On the next screen, enter the name you want shown on your dashboard (for example, Sarah Johnson).
        3. Set a password of at least 6 characters.
        4. Click **Create account →**.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    <Info>
      If you see "Looks like you already have an account. Sign in below," you have signed up before. Use the sign-in form on the same page, or click **Forgot Password?** to reset your credentials.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    The **Pick a plan** page shows three cards. Every plan starts with the same $1 three-day trial, so choose the plan you want to be on once you are actively making videos — not just the cheapest one to test with.

    | Plan | Monthly | Yearly (per mo) | Avatars | Credits/mo | Videos/mo |
    |------|---------|-----------------|---------|------------|-----------|
    | **Starter** | $39 | $31 | 1 | 325 | 30 |
    | **Creator** | $99 | $79 | 3 | 790 | 150 |
    | **Influencer** | from $249 | from $199 | Unlimited | 1,490–5,990 | 500 |

    <Note>
      **Creator is the plan most creators end up on.** It gives you three avatars (one per niche), HD image resolution, auto-publishing on a schedule, the Trending Intel dashboard, and reel-to-video ingestion — the full toolkit for growing a channel. Starter is best if you want to test the platform with a single avatar before committing to a higher tier.
    </Note>

    1. Use the **Monthly / Annual** toggle at the top of the page. Annual billing saves 20%.
    2. On the Influencer card, open the credit tier dropdown to pick 1,490, 2,490, 3,990, or 5,990 credits per month.
    3. Click **Start trial for $1** on the card you want.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your card">
    The **Set up payment** page has two sections: **Payment Method** and **Order Summary**.

    1. Enter your card details in the secure form. Avatar Factory never sees or stores your card number.
    2. Confirm the plan and billing interval in the **Order Summary**. The **Due Today** line shows **$1**.
    3. If you have a promo code, click **Promo code?**, paste it, and click **Apply**.
    4. Click **Start 3-Day Trial for $1** to confirm.

    <Warning>
      If you see "Your card could not be charged. Please try again," your bank declined the charge. Try a different card or contact your bank, then try again. If you see "Card confirmed but we could not start your trial. Contact support," write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with your account email and the team will resolve it manually.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer four onboarding questions">
    After payment, four screens appear — one question each. Every question has a **Skip** option, so none are required.

<Accordion title="Question 1 — Do you already have an AI influencer?">
  Pick **"I already have an AI influencer / avatar"** or **"It's my first AI influencer / avatar"**. If you already have one, you can paste a link to it. This tells the app whether to point your Getting Started checklist at the avatar builder or the import flow.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Question 2 — What niche are you building in?">
  Pick a tile. Your selection becomes your default category across the app — templates, followed accounts, and the Viral Radar dashboard all adapt to it. The 18+ niche asks you to confirm your age before it appears.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Question 3 — How did you hear about us?">
  Pick a source and click **Continue →**. This helps the team understand which channels are working; it has no effect on your account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Question 4 — What are you looking for?">
  Pick one of five goals: promote a physical or e-commerce product, promote an app, promote digital products, affiliate marketing, or other. After selecting a goal you can add a short note and a link to what you are promoting — both optional. Click **Finish Setup →**.
</Accordion>
  </Step>

</Steps>

## What you see after onboarding

You land on the **Getting Started** checklist, which has three items:

1. **Create Avatar** — opens the avatar wizard (Identity → Story → Face → Voice).
2. **Generate** — opens the video wizard once you have a saved avatar.
3. **Add Accounts** — connects your social accounts for auto-publishing.

<Tip>
  Start with **Create Avatar**. The avatar is the foundation everything else builds on, and the trial's 15 credits are sized to complete exactly one full avatar build. Head to the [Quick Start guide](/quickstart) for the full walkthrough.
</Tip>

If you told the onboarding flow that you already have an avatar, the checklist shows **Import your avatar** instead, so you can bring your own face and skip the generation steps.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Can I change my plan later?">
  Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades are scheduled for the start of your next billing cycle so you keep your current credits until the period ends. On the Influencer plan you can move between credit tiers (1,490 / 2,490 / 3,990 / 5,990) without changing plans. Head to **Settings → Billing** to make any change.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if my card is declined?">
  Your bank declined the charge — this is not an Avatar Factory error. The most common causes are insufficient funds, a card with international transactions disabled, or a prepaid card that doesn't support recurring billing. Try a different card or contact your bank to authorize the charge, then return to [app.avatarfactory.io/register](https://app.avatarfactory.io/register) and start again. If you see "Card confirmed but we could not start your trial," email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with your account email and the team will finish the setup manually.
</Accordion>
