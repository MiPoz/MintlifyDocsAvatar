Avatar Factory is an all-in-one AI influencer studio. You describe a character, pick a niche, and the platform generates a realistic face, matches it with a voice, writes scripts from proven viral hooks, and renders finished short-form reels — all without any filming, recording, or editing on your part.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.avatarfactory.io/register](https://app.avatarfactory.io/register). Every plan starts with a 3-day trial for $1 — no commitment required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build an avatar">
    Design a character from scratch or pick from a pre-built template. The wizard walks you through identity, backstory, face generation, and voice selection. Your first avatar costs about 10 credits and takes under 5 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first video">
    Open the video wizard, choose a source (AI brainstorm, reel URL, or your own script), and click **Generate**. Your reel renders in the background and lands in your Videos tab — typically in 10–20 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grow with data">
    Track competitor accounts, surface viral reels with Viral Radar, and clone high-performing content directly into the video wizard — all backed by 500M+ analyzed videos.
  </Step>
</Steps>
