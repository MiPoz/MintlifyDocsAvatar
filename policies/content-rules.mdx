## Copyright complaints (Terms, section 10)

If you believe content on the Service infringes your copyright, email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with all five of the following items — we need all five before we can act on a notice:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the copyrighted work">
    Describe or link to the work you believe is being infringed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify the infringing material">
    Provide the location of the allegedly infringing material on Avatar Factory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include your contact details">
    Provide your name, address, phone number, and email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="State good-faith belief">
    Include a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign under penalty of perjury">
    Include a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the copyright owner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A likeness or consent complaint goes to the same address with a link to the content in question.
