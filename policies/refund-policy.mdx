This page explains the Avatar Factory refund policy in plain language. The policy itself lives in the [Terms of Service](https://avatarfactory.io/terms), sections 3.3 and 4. The quotes below are copied from that page word for word. If this page and the Terms ever differ, the Terms win.

## Refund windows by charge type

| Charge | Refund window |
|---|---|
| Monthly plan payment | 24 hours from payment |
| Annual plan payment | 7 days from payment |
| Credit purchase | 24 hours from payment (credits must be unused) |
| Add-on | No refund |
| Any charge older than 7 days | No refund |

## The policy, quoted

From section 3.3, Cancellation:

> "You can cancel your subscription at any time online from your account settings, with no more effort than it took to subscribe. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing term you have already paid for, and you keep access until then. Except as stated in the Refund Policy below, we do not provide prorated refunds for unused time."

From section 4, Refund Policy:

> "Refund requests must be submitted within the window that applies to the charge, measured from the moment of payment. No refund request will be accepted more than seven days after a charge. These windows apply to renewal charges as well: each renewal starts its own window."

> "Monthly plans. Eligible for a refund if the request is made within 24 hours of payment."

> "Annual plans. Eligible for a refund if the request is made within 7 days of payment."

> "Add-ons. Non-refundable."

> "Credits. Eligible for a refund if the request is made within 24 hours of payment and the credits have not yet been used."

> "Credits that have been used, and services that have been consumed, are not refundable. Approved refunds may be reduced by payment processing and transaction fees of up to 15%. Refunds are issued to the original payment method, and the time it takes to appear depends on your payment provider or bank."

## Key rules in plain words

**The clock starts at the moment of payment.** Not when you noticed the charge or when you last used the app. Find the exact timestamp in **Plan & Usage → Billing History**.

**Every charge starts its own window.** A monthly renewal is a new charge — it is eligible for a refund within 24 hours of that specific renewal payment.

**Used credits are not refundable.** A credit purchase becomes ineligible the moment any of those credits are used. A plan or service you have already used is not refunded.

**Approved refunds may be reduced by up to 15%** for payment processing and transaction fees. Refunds are returned to the original payment method. How long the refund takes to appear depends on your bank.

**Canceling and refunding are different actions.** Canceling stops future charges and you keep access until the end of the term you paid for. It does not by itself trigger a refund for the current term. If you want both, cancel in the app and submit a refund request within the window.

**A failed render is not a refund case.** Credits held for a failed job are returned to your balance automatically — that is an in-app credit remedy, not a money refund. See [Credits and Limits](/troubleshooting/credits-and-limits) for details.

## How to request a refund

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the charge">
    Open **Plan & Usage** → scroll to **Billing History** → note the exact date and amount of the charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email support">
    Write to [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) from the email address on your account. Include the charge date, the amount, and one line explaining why you are requesting a refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for a reply">
    Support replies within one business day. A refund is confirmed in writing before it is issued.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What support cannot do

Support cannot extend refund windows and cannot refund credits that have already been used. If your request falls outside the policy, support will say so plainly and point you to alternatives — such as a plan downgrade scheduled for the end of your term, or the free 30-day pause.

<Warning>
  Contact Avatar Factory support **before** filing a dispute with your bank. From section 4.1 of the Terms: filing a chargeback without contacting us first, or filing one that is determined to be fraudulent or without merit, may result in suspension or termination of your account and forfeiture of remaining credits. Avatar Factory reserves the right to dispute chargebacks and to provide records of your account and usage to the payment processor.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long do I have to request a refund?">
    It depends on the charge. A monthly plan payment is eligible within 24 hours. An annual plan payment is eligible within 7 days. A credit purchase is eligible within 24 hours, only if the credits are unused. No request is accepted more than seven days after any charge. Each renewal starts its own window.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund if I forgot to cancel before the renewal?">
    A monthly renewal starts a fresh 24-hour window. If you request a refund within that window, the renewal charge is eligible. Annual renewals have a 7-day window. Check your **Billing History** for the exact payment timestamp.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I get money back if a render fails?">
    No — but credits are returned automatically. A failed render releases its credit hold back to your balance in-app. That is not a money refund; it is a credit remedy. Your plan payment is separate. See the [Render Failures](/troubleshooting/render-failures) page for how released credits work.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
