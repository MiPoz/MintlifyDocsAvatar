Avatar Factory is an all-in-one AI influencer video studio. You describe a character, the platform builds a face and voice for it, and the video wizard turns your scripts into ready-to-post short-form reels. You never step in front of a camera, record audio, or touch a timeline editor — you write or generate the script, set the shots, and the render runs in the background.

## The four parts of an avatar

Every avatar you build has four components. The avatar wizard walks you through them in order:

| Step | Component | What it defines |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Identity** | Age, gender, country, look, vibe, reference images, and niche category |
| 2 | **Story** | A backstory and a target audience (the AI can draft both) |
| 3 | **Face** | Three generated face candidates — pick one, refine it, or generate more |
| 4 | **Voice** | A voice description, three audio previews, and one selection |

After you save, Avatar Factory automatically builds a **Reference Pack** — six angles of the same face — that keeps your avatar visually consistent across every scene and video.

<Tip>
  If you already have a character, use **Import your avatar** to upload your own photo and bring your own voice. The wizard skips the generation steps and locks your face directly.
</Tip>
