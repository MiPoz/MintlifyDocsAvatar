This guide walks you through everything from creating your account to a rendered reel. The trial covers the avatar build; your first video renders on the paid plan that activates after the 3-day trial window. You need an email address and a payment card to begin.

<Note>
  The $1 three-day trial includes 15 credits — exactly enough to build one complete avatar (face candidates, voice previews, and reference pack). Video rendering uses your plan's monthly credits, not the trial credits, so start the render once your paid plan is active.
</Note>

## Before you start

- **An email address** you can access, or a Google or Meta account for one-click sign-in.
- **A payment card.** The trial charges $1 today and auto-converts to your chosen plan after 72 hours. Cancel anytime from your billing page.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.avatarfactory.io/register](https://app.avatarfactory.io/register). You have three sign-in options:

    - **Continue with Google** — one click, no password needed.
    - **Continue with Meta** — one click using your Facebook account.
    - **Continue with email** — enter your email, then set a display name and a password of at least 6 characters on the next screen.

    If you see "Looks like you already have an account", use the sign-in form on the same page or click **Forgot Password?** to reset your credentials.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a plan and start the trial">
    The **Pick a plan** page shows three options. Every plan starts with the same $1 three-day trial — choose the plan you want to be on once you are actively making videos.

    | Plan | Monthly price | Avatars | Monthly credits | Best for |
    |------|--------------|---------|-----------------|----------|
    | **Starter** | $39/mo | 1 | 325 | Testing with one avatar and one channel |
    | **Creator** | $99/mo | 3 | 790 | Most creators — HD, auto-publish, trending intel |
    | **Influencer** | from $249/mo | Unlimited | 1,490–5,990 | Agencies and multi-niche operators |

    1. Use the **Monthly / Annual** toggle — annual billing saves 20%.
    2. Click **Start trial for $1** on the plan you want.
    3. Enter your card on the **Set up payment** page. The **Due Today** line shows $1.
    4. Click **Start 3-Day Trial for $1** to confirm.

    <Tip>
      Not sure which plan to pick? Creator is the plan most creators end up on: three avatars, HD images, auto-publishing, and trending intel. You can switch plans at any time from your Billing page.
    </Tip>

    <Warning>
      If you see "Your card could not be charged. Please try again," your bank declined the $1 charge. Try a different card or contact your bank, then retry. If you see "Card confirmed but we could not start your trial. Contact support," email [Support@AvatarFactory.IO](mailto:Support@AvatarFactory.IO) with your account email.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build your first avatar">
    After payment, four quick onboarding questions appear (all skippable). You then land on the **Getting Started** checklist. Click **Create Avatar** to open the avatar wizard.

    The wizard has four steps — **Identity → Story → Face → Voice**:

    1. **Identity:** Select a featured template in your niche (or start from scratch). The fields are pre-filled; read them once and click **Continue to story →**.
    2. **Story:** Click **Ideate for me** under Backstory, pick an angle, then click **Generate** under Target audience and pick one. Click **Continue to face previews →**. This triggers face generation and uses your first credits (1 credit per face, 3 faces = 3 credits).
    3. **Face:** Three face tiles render in about 75 seconds. Name the avatar in the **Name** field while you wait. Click **Choose** on the face you like. Refine or generate more faces if needed (1 credit per refine, 3 credits for a new set). Click **Continue to voice →**.
    4. **Voice:** Click **Generate 3 voice previews** (1 credit for all three). Play each preview and click **Use this voice** on your pick. Click **Finish avatar →**.

    Saving the avatar automatically builds the **Reference Pack** (6 credit charge), locking the face at six angles for consistent video rendering.

    **Approximate credit cost for one complete avatar build:**

    | Action | Credits used |
    |--------|-------------|
    | 3 face candidates | 3 cr |
    | 3 voice previews | 1 cr |
    | Reference pack (on save) | 6 cr |
    | **Total** | **~10 cr** |

    <Note>
      The trial's 15 credits cover one full avatar build with a few credits to spare for a face refine or a second voice batch.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make your first video">
    From the avatar done screen, click **Make your first video** (or open **Generate** in the sidebar). The video wizard has five steps — **Avatar → Source → Script → Shots → Render**:

    1. **Avatar:** Click your avatar tile and click **Continue to source →**.
    2. **Source:** Choose where your script comes from. For a first video, select **Ideate with AI content expert** — the AI brainstorms six viral hooks for your niche. Pick one and click **Continue to script →**.
    3. **Script:** Review and edit the generated draft in the script editor. Read it aloud once — this is exactly what your avatar will say. Click **Continue** when ready.
    4. **Shots:** The wizard splits your script into shots. For each shot, choose the type:
       - **Talking-head** — avatar faces the camera and speaks (1 credit/second, powered by HeyGen Avatar IV).
       - **Cinematic motion** — avatar moves through a scene (2 credits/second, powered by Kling v3).

       Assign a scene to each shot that starts a new setting. Click **Generate** when the shot list is set.
    5. **Render:** A confirmation shows the total seconds, credit cost, and remaining balance. Click **Generate · N cr** to start.

    **Credit cost reference:**

    | Action | Credits |
    |--------|---------|
    | Generated image | 1 cr |
    | Voice preview batch (3 previews) | 1 cr |
    | Reference pack | 6 cr |
    | Standard video (talking-head) | 1 cr / second |
    | Cinematic video | 2 cr / second |

    <Info>
      On the Starter plan, videos are capped at 45 seconds and use the talking-head model only. Cinematic motion shots and videos longer than 45 seconds are available on Creator and above.
    </Info>

    Rendering runs in the background — close the tab if you want. When the render completes, you receive an email notification and an in-app alert. Find the finished reel in the **Videos** tab, ready to download or publish.
  </Step>

</Steps>

## What happens after the render

Once your video lands in the **Videos** tab you can:

- **Download** the reel as an MP4.
- **Review captions** and edit any line before publishing.
- **Regenerate a shot** if one scene didn't come out right, without re-rendering the whole video.
- **Publish directly** to Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts (Creator and Influencer plans).
